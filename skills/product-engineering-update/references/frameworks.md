## Slide 1: The answer

- [Engineering Management, Pyramid Communication: An Actionable Guide](https://engmanage.substack.com/p/pyramid-communication) (Nov 2025). Lead with the recommendation, then the supporting arguments, then the data.
- [Cedric Chin, The Amazon Weekly Business Review](https://commoncog.com/the-amazon-weekly-business-review/) (Jul 2024). A weekly metrics review works by exposing cause and effect, and depends on leadership discipline more than tooling.
- [Amanda Herson, Founder Collective, Debates Over Updates](https://foundercollective.com/?p=14226) (Mar 2026). Start with what changed since the last meeting, send data ahead, and build the meeting around 1 decision.
- [Product School, Product Leadership](https://productschool.com/blog/leadership/product-leadership) (Nov 2026). One story cut three ways, why this problem for teams, why this bet for executives, why now for the board.

<a id="s02"></a>
## Slide 2: Strategy on a page

- [Will Larson, Introduction for Engineering Strategy](https://lethain.com/intro-eng-strategy/) (May 2024). Diagnosis, guiding policy and coherent actions, applied to an engineering organisation.
- [Lenny Rachitsky with Chandra Janakiraman, An operator's guide to product strategy](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/an-operators-guide-to-product-strategy-chandra-janakiraman) (Jan 2025). A good strategy names a few focus areas and the areas explicitly out of focus.
- [Roger Martin, Why the How-to-Win Strategy Choice is So Hard](https://rogerlmartin.substack.com/p/why-the-how-to-win-strategy-choice) (Nov 2025). Choose where to play and how to win together. A list of initiatives is not a strategy.
- Claire Vo, The Last Roadmap (Lenny and Friends Summit, Sep 2026). Hold convictions for years and treat features as disposable.

<a id="s03"></a>
## Slide 3: Line of sight

- [John Cutler, Stop the (Goal) Cascade Madness](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-305-stop-the-goal-cascade-madness) (Aug 2024). Many goal cascades are org chart cascades with no model underneath. 2 levels of goals are enough.
- [Teresa Torres, When It Comes to Discovery, Something is Better Than Nothing](https://www.producttalk.org/y-oslo-2024/) (Dec 2024). An opportunity solution tree maps the path from an outcome through opportunities and solutions to tested assumptions.
- [Linear, Initiative updates](https://linear.app/changelog/2025-02-13-initiative-updates) (Feb 2025). Written updates report progress and summarise work across the projects in an initiative.
- [Notion on Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/notion) (2026). The team that owns a capability also owns the tool its agents use.

<a id="s04"></a>
## Slide 4: Portfolio

- [Kent Beck, Canon 3X: Explore/Expand/Extract](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/canon-3x-exploreexpandextract) (Jul 2026). Explore, expand and extract each need their own approach, finance included. Running one phase by another's rules kills ideas.
- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (2024). Roadmap and adoption shown by product.

<a id="s05"></a>
## Slide 5: Investment mix

- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (Jul 2024). Capacity across new capabilities, quality, internal productivity and keep-the-lights-on, shown for last, current and next quarters.
- [Adam Ferrari, Allocating with Intent](https://adamferrari.substack.com/p/allocating-with-intent) (Mar 2025). Every R&D organisation is placing bets. Name the categories, set target percentages and track actual spend against them.
- [John Cutler, Time Allocation ≠ Capacity Allocation](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-377-time-allocation-capacity) (Sep 2025). Hours allocated are not capacity. Interruptions, rework and dependencies shape what a team can deliver.
- [LinearB, engineering benchmarks 2025](https://linearb.io/blog/engineering-benchmarks-2025-devex-developer-productivity) (Jan 2025, about 3,000 organisations). Effort averages 55% new value, 20% enhancements, 15% developer experience and 10% keeping the lights on. LinearB presents these as guidance.
- [SaaS Capital, Spending Benchmarks for Private B2B SaaS Companies](https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/spending-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-saas-companies/) (Jun 2026, survey of more than 1,000 companies). The median spend on research and development is 22% of ARR.

## Slide 6: What we learned

- Teresa Torres, Continuous Discovery Habits (2021). In 2026 she said she would add a chapter on evals as a discovery habit, and that cheap building is no reason to build everything ([airfocus, Jun 2026](https://airfocus.com/blog/how-ai-is-changing-product-management/)).
- [Marty Cagan, The AI Productivity Paradox](https://www.svpg.com/the-ai-productivity-paradox/) (Jul 2026). Build to learn in discovery, build to earn in delivery. His four risks are value, usability, feasibility and viability.
- [Hamel Husain and Shreya Shankar on error analysis](https://hamel.dev/notes/llm/ai-product-engineering/evals-error-analysis.html) (Jun 2026). A product manager or domain expert should read the failures.
- [First Round, The Art of Evals at Figma](https://review.firstround.com/figma-ai-eval-process/) (May 2025). People at the centre of AI product evaluation.

## Slide 7: Customer & market signal

- Melissa Perri and Denise Tilles, Product Operations (2023). Business data, customer data, process.
- [Melissa Perri, The bottleneck isn't building, it's deciding](https://productinstitute.kit.com/posts/the-bottleneck-isn-t-building-it-s-deciding) (2026), with the State of AI in Product 2026 survey of 309 product leaders.

## Slide 8: Shipped vs impact

- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (2024). Product spotlight with success measures defined up front.
- [Karri Saarinen, 10 rules for crafting products](https://www.figma.com/blog/karri-saarinens-10-rules-for-crafting-products-that-stand-out/) (Mar 2025). Reduce scope to raise quality.

<a id="s09"></a>
## Slide 9: Delivery system health

- [DORA, State of AI-assisted Software Development](https://dora.dev/dora-report-2025/) (Sep 2025, about 5,000 respondents). AI use now tracks with higher throughput and lower stability.
- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (2024). The scorecard layout here, with speed, effectiveness, quality and impact.
- [DX Core 4 and the AI Measurement Framework](https://newsletter.getdx.com/p/measuring-engineering-productivity-in-the-ai-era) (2025). Utilization, impact, cost.
- [Faros AI, AI Productivity Paradox](https://243608892.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na2.net/hubfs/243608892/AI_Engineering_Impact_Report_July_2025_Faros_AI.pdf) (Jul 2025, 10,000+ developers). 98% more merged pull requests, 91% longer reviews, no company-level gain. Vendor telemetry.

<a id="s10"></a>
## Slide 10: Platform & security

- [Will Larson, Steps to build an engineering strategy](https://lethain.com/components-of-eng-strategy/) (Mar 2025). Explore, diagnose, refine, set policy, then run it. Each step feeds the next.
- [InfoQ, Grafana applies SLOs and error budgets to AI agent behaviour](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/grafana-slo-ai-agent/) (Oct 2026). Service-level objectives and error budgets can track agent quality, because a system can stay up while it becomes less trustworthy.
- [InfoQ on Joy Ebertz, All Tech Debt is Not Created Equal](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/03/tech-debt-not-equal/) (QCon London, Mar 2026). Decide which debt matters with 6 questions, starting with the cost of doing nothing.
- [Product School, AI-native](https://productschool.com/blog/artificial-intelligence/ai-native) (Feb 2026). Version prompts and models, log behaviour, keep an incident process for AI features.

<a id="s11"></a>
## Slide 11: Outcome scorecard

- [Aleph and Benchmarkit, 2026 SaaS & AI Performance Benchmarks](https://www.getaleph.com/answers/net-revenue-retention-saas-2026) (Jun 2026, 342 companies, 2025 actuals). Source of the retention benchmark.
- [Eric Weber, North star metrics for AI data products](https://ericdataproduct.substack.com/p/north-star-metrics-for-ai-data-products) (May 2026). An AI product's North Star measures the change in its users' downstream work. Adoption, token use and satisfaction don't qualify.
- Christina Wodtke, Radical Focus (2nd ed. 2021). A light cadence for goals. Linear prefers strategy and a North Star to team-level OKRs.
- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (2024). Report adoption over several periods, never as a single point.
- [Atlassian, State of Teams 2026](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/how-we-build/the-future-of-product-craft) (May 2026). 89% of executives say AI sped work up and 6% can point to company-wide return.

## Slide 12: AI in the product

- [ICONIQ, State of AI 2026](https://www.iconiq.com/growth/reports/state-of-ai-2026) (Jul 2026, survey of about 300 executives). AI product gross margin averaged 45% in 2025. The 2026 and 2027 figures are projections.
- [Husain and Shankar, error analysis](https://hamel.dev/notes/llm/ai-product-engineering/evals-error-analysis.html) (Jun 2026). Read failures first, then write checks.
- [Kyle Poyar, State of B2B Monetization 2025](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2025-state-of-b2b-monetization) (240 companies). Seat pricing fell from 21% to 15% and hybrid rose from 27% to 41%.
- [Product School, AI-native](https://productschool.com/blog/artificial-intelligence/ai-native) (Feb 2026). The remove-it test, and evals as a condition for shipping.

## Slide 13: AI in how we build

- [METR's 2025 trial](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) (Jul 2025, 16 developers, 246 issues) found experienced developers took 19% longer with AI. In [Feb 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/) METR called that result out of date and changed its experiment design.
- [Stripe, Minions](https://stripe.dev/blog/minions-stripes-one-shot-end-to-end-coding-agents) (2026). More than 1,300 merged pull requests a week with no human-written code, every one reviewed by a person.
- Ramp CPO Geoff Charles (Sep 2026) put agent-written pull requests at 75% and called speed a systems problem. A Ramp data engineer described internal AI return as measured by "vibes" ([dbt Summit 2026](https://www.getdbt.com/blog/ramp-internal-ai-agents-million-sessions)).
- [Vercel Ship 2026](https://vercel.com/blog/vercel-ship-2026-recap) (Oct 2026). Agents that find and fix bugs from production context.
- [Shopify's AI memo](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/04/08/internal-memo-shopify-ceo-declares-ai-non-optional/) (Apr 2025). The best-known example of an explicit AI stance, which DORA lists as a capability.

<a id="s14"></a>
## Slide 14: Risks & assumptions

- McKinsey, A new operating model for a new world (Jun 2025). 12 elements and 4 outcomes, clarity, speed, skills and commitment. It reports a 30% gap between strategy potential and delivery, even at strong companies.
- [Cagan, Build vs Buy in the Age of AI](https://www.svpg.com/the-ai-productivity-paradox/) and related SVPG pieces (2025 to 2026).
- [Robert Kimani, LeadDev, Your engineering strategy has a new failure mode](https://leaddev.com/leadership/your-engineering-strategy-has-a-new-failure-mode) (Oct 2026). Match how reversible a commitment is to how strong its evidence is. Platform migrations can take years to reverse.

- [Chandra Janakiraman on Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/an-operators-guide-to-product-strategy-chandra-janakiraman) (Jan 2025). Name the areas that are explicitly out of focus.

## Slide 16: Next bets

- [Lenny Rachitsky with Ryan Singer, A better way to plan, build, and ship products](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/shape-up-ryan-singer) (Mar 2025) and [John Cutler on Shape Up's constraints](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-386-understanding-enabling-constraints) (Nov 2025). Appetite, bets, fixed time with variable scope, and a circuit breaker.
- Claire Vo, The Last Roadmap (Sep 2026). Features as probes, experiments or promises, with commitment stated plainly. See [ChatPRD](https://www.chatprd.ai).
- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (2024). Show the next 12 months and a longer view.

## Slide 17: Team & operating model

- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (2024). Forward org chart, open leadership roles, expertise gaps.
- Marty Cagan, Transformed (2024) and [A Vision for Product Teams](https://www.svpg.com/a-vision-for-product-teams/) (Feb 2025).
- McKinsey, A new operating model for a new world (Jun 2025). Structure is 1 of 12 elements.
- [Figma, 2026 AI report](https://www.figma.com/blog/2026-ai-report/) (Jun 2026). Developers taking part in design rose from 44% to 60% in a year.
- Lenny Rachitsky with Cat Wu (Apr 2026) on how Anthropic's product team ships in days, and [The Pragmatic Engineer on Claude Code](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/how-claude-code-is-built).

## Slide 18: Decisions & asks

- [First Round Review, Board Meetings 101](https://review.firstround.com/board-meetings-101-for-early-stage-founders/) (Aug 2026). "Thoughts?" is a weak ask. Say whether you want advice, pressure-testing, introductions or a formal decision.
- [Melissa Perri](https://productinstitute.kit.com/posts/the-bottleneck-isn-t-building-it-s-deciding) (2026). Deciding is now the constraint, so measure product work by decisions changed.
