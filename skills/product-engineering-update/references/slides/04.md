# Slide 4: Portfolio

Act: Strategy. Audiences: Leadership, Staff.

## The question it answers

Across every product line, where does the money go and what does each line return?

## Required data

Capacity and revenue split by product line, one lead outcome per line, a stage for each.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Issue tracker | Capacity by product line | Yes |
| Finance actuals | ARR by product line | Yes |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `table`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

One row per line and one lead outcome per row. Judge each line by its stage. With a single product, drop this slide.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Sources: Linear capacity by product line (includes each line's keep-the-lights-on work), Finance ARR by SKU. Q3 FY26."

## Titles

- Good: "The agent line produced 81% of ARR growth on 34% of capacity." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Portfolio" (a label; it claims nothing).
