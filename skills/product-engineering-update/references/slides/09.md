# Slide 9: Delivery system health

Act: Delivery. Audiences: Leadership.

## The question it answers

Is the system that ships our product healthy and stable?

## Required data

Source control and CI data, incident records, a developer survey. Pick 8 to 10 metrics and keep them every period.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Source control and CI | Deploys, lead time, review time and change failures | Yes |
| Incidents and status | Time to restore | Yes |
| Developer survey | The developer experience index | No |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `scorecard`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

No speed metric without its quality pair. Show a healthy range for each metric, because single-point targets on delivery metrics get gamed. Define every index. Leave out story points, velocity, lines of code and AI suggestion acceptance rate.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Sources: GitHub, CI, incident tool, quarterly developer survey. Q3 FY26."

## Titles

- Good: "Throughput is up 21%. Review time and change failures are the cost." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Delivery system health" (a label; it claims nothing).

## Reading

- [DORA, State of AI-assisted Software Development](https://dora.dev/dora-report-2025/) (Sep 2025, about 5,000 respondents). AI use now tracks with higher throughput and lower stability.
- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (2024). The scorecard layout here, with speed, effectiveness, quality and impact.
- [DX Core 4 and the AI Measurement Framework](https://newsletter.getdx.com/p/measuring-engineering-productivity-in-the-ai-era) (2025). Utilization, impact, cost.
- [Faros AI, AI Productivity Paradox](https://243608892.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na2.net/hubfs/243608892/AI_Engineering_Impact_Report_July_2025_Faros_AI.pdf) (Jul 2025, 10,000+ developers). 98% more merged pull requests, 91% longer reviews, no company-level gain. Vendor telemetry.
