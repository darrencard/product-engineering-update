# Slide 2: Strategy on a page

Act: Strategy. Audiences: Leadership, Staff.

## The question it answers

What is our bet and what are we not doing?

## Required data

The strategy document. If it doesn't exist, the intake interview writes this slide with you.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Intake interview | Objectives, diagnosis, guiding policy, actions and non-goals | Yes |
| Documents | The strategy document | No |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `cards`, `table`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

The "we will not" column is required. Test it by asking 2 engineers and a salesperson to explain the strategy in 2 sentences each.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Strategy last reviewed Jul 2026. Next review Jan 2027."

## Titles

- Good: "We win mid-market freight brokers by automating the exception, priced per load." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Strategy on a page" (a label; it claims nothing).

## Reading

- [Will Larson, Introduction for Engineering Strategy](https://lethain.com/intro-eng-strategy/) (May 2024). Diagnosis, guiding policy and coherent actions, applied to an engineering organisation.
- [Lenny Rachitsky with Chandra Janakiraman, An operator's guide to product strategy](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/an-operators-guide-to-product-strategy-chandra-janakiraman) (Jan 2025). A good strategy names a few focus areas and the areas explicitly out of focus.
- [Roger Martin, Why the How-to-Win Strategy Choice is So Hard](https://rogerlmartin.substack.com/p/why-the-how-to-win-strategy-choice) (Nov 2025). Choose where to play and how to win together. A list of initiatives is not a strategy.
- Claire Vo, The Last Roadmap (Lenny and Friends Summit, Sep 2026). Hold convictions for years and treat features as disposable.
