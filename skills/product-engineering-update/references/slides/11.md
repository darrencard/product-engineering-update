# Slide 11: Outcome scorecard

Act: Measurement. Audiences: Leadership, Board, Staff.

## The question it answers

Are customer and business outcomes moving?

## Required data

One North Star, 3 to 5 input metrics, each with a definition, an owner, a target and a prior period.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Product analytics | One North Star and 3 to 5 input metrics, each with a definition, owner, target and prior period | Yes |
| Billing | Retention and expansion ARR | Yes |
| Warehouse | The query behind each metric | No |
| CRM | Customer accounts behind the retention measures | No |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `scorecard`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

Same metrics every period. Add an external benchmark only where a sourced one exists, and write "none" where it doesn't. Split agent activity from human activity wherever agents act for customers.

The "At this pace (Projected)" note under the scorecard lists each outcome not yet at its target: this period's change, repeated until the target is met, in periods, and before or after the target's date when it has one. It never sits in the scorecard, and a title never states it as a result.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Sources: PostHog, warehouse, Stripe, CRM. Quarter ending 30 Sep 2026."

## Titles

- Good: "The North Star beat target. Activation and expansion did not." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Outcome scorecard" (a label; it claims nothing).

## Reading

- [Aleph and Benchmarkit, 2026 SaaS & AI Performance Benchmarks](https://www.getaleph.com/answers/net-revenue-retention-saas-2026) (Jun 2026, 342 companies, 2025 actuals). Source of the retention benchmark.
- [Eric Weber, North star metrics for AI data products](https://ericdataproduct.substack.com/p/north-star-metrics-for-ai-data-products) (May 2026). An AI product's North Star measures the change in its users' downstream work. Adoption, token use and satisfaction don't qualify.
- Christina Wodtke, Radical Focus (2nd ed. 2021). A light cadence for goals. Linear prefers strategy and a North Star to team-level OKRs.
- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (2024). Report adoption over several periods, never as a single point.
- [Atlassian, State of Teams 2026](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/how-we-build/the-future-of-product-craft) (May 2026). 89% of executives say AI sped work up and 6% can point to company-wide return.
