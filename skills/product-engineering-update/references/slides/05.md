# Slide 5: Investment mix

Act: Strategy. Audiences: Leadership, Board.

## The question it answers

Where did the people and the money go, and did that match the strategy?

## Required data

A work-type label on every issue or epic, Finance actuals, model provider and tooling invoices.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Issue tracker | A work-type label on every issue or epic | Yes |
| Finance actuals | Plan and actual spend, with model provider and tooling invoices | Yes |
| Finance actuals | Cash balance, net burn and runway | No |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `plan_vs_actual`, `table`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

Explain any variance over 3 points in one sentence. Show token and tooling spend next to headcount, and R&D as a share of revenue with a dated path. Agree the split with Finance before the meeting.

When the intake names `capitalisation.work_types` and `capitalisation.cost_lines`, the slide adds a capitalisable estimate: the share of the team's time on those work types, applied to those cost lines. It is always labelled an estimate for finance to confirm (`capex-not-estimate` blocks one that is not), and it says who confirmed it, or that nobody has yet.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Sources: Linear work-type labels, Finance actuals, provider invoices. Quarter ending 30 Sep 2026."

## Titles

- Good: "We planned 15% on keeping the lights on and spent 22%. Inference ran 28% over plan." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Investment mix" (a label; it claims nothing).

## Reading

- ICONIQ Growth, The Essential R&D Board Slides (Jul 2024). Capacity across new capabilities, quality, internal productivity and keep-the-lights-on, shown for last, current and next quarters.
- [Adam Ferrari, Allocating with Intent](https://adamferrari.substack.com/p/allocating-with-intent) (Mar 2025). Every R&D organisation is placing bets. Name the categories, set target percentages and track actual spend against them.
- [John Cutler, Time Allocation ≠ Capacity Allocation](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-377-time-allocation-capacity) (Sep 2025). Hours allocated are not capacity. Interruptions, rework and dependencies shape what a team can deliver.
- [LinearB, engineering benchmarks 2025](https://linearb.io/blog/engineering-benchmarks-2025-devex-developer-productivity) (Jan 2025, about 3,000 organisations). Effort averages 55% new value, 20% enhancements, 15% developer experience and 10% keeping the lights on. LinearB presents these as guidance.
- [SaaS Capital, Spending Benchmarks for Private B2B SaaS Companies](https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/spending-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-saas-companies/) (Jun 2026, survey of more than 1,000 companies). The median spend on research and development is 22% of ARR.
