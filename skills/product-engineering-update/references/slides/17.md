# Slide 17: Team & operating model

Act: Next investments. Audiences: Leadership, Staff.

## The question it answers

Is the team shaped for how we now build, and where do we need help?

## Required data

Headcount against plan by function, open senior roles, attrition, a forward 12-month view.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| HR and hiring | Headcount against plan by function, open senior roles and attrition | Yes |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `table`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

Focus on the hard hires. Name the help you want from the reader. For staff, add recognition and drop the ratios.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Sources: HR system, hiring tracker. As of 30 Sep 2026."

## Titles

- Good: "60 of 64 planned roles are filled. Head of AI Quality is the hire that matters." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Team & operating model" (a label; it claims nothing).
