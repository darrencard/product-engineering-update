# Slide 3: Line of sight

Act: Strategy. Audiences: Leadership, Staff.

## The question it answers

How does each team's work connect to what the company is trying to do?

## Required data

Initiatives or epics tagged to an objective, with an owner. Agents that carry work are listed as owners too.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Issue tracker | Initiatives or epics tagged to an objective, each with an owner | Yes |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `tree`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

Each row states the cause in one sentence and a confidence level. A branch with no stated cause is an org chart, so it comes off the slide.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Source: Linear initiatives tagged by objective, Q3 FY26."

## Titles

- Good: "78% of R&D capacity traces to a company objective through a stated cause." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Line of sight" (a label; it claims nothing).

## Reading

- [John Cutler, Stop the (Goal) Cascade Madness](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-305-stop-the-goal-cascade-madness) (Aug 2024). Many goal cascades are org chart cascades with no model underneath. 2 levels of goals are enough.
- [Teresa Torres, When It Comes to Discovery, Something is Better Than Nothing](https://www.producttalk.org/y-oslo-2024/) (Dec 2024). An opportunity solution tree maps the path from an outcome through opportunities and solutions to tested assumptions.
- [Linear, Initiative updates](https://linear.app/changelog/2025-02-13-initiative-updates) (Feb 2025). Written updates report progress and summarise work across the projects in an initiative.
- [Notion on Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/notion) (2026). The team that owns a capability also owns the tool its agents use.
