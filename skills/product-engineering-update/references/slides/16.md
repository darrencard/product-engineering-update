# Slide 16: Next bets

Act: Next investments. Audiences: Leadership, Board, Staff.

## The question it answers

What do we fund next and what do we expect back?

## Required data

A one-page brief per bet with appetite, expected result, confidence and a stop rule. Each bet traces to slide 3 and to evidence on slide 6.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Documents | A one-page brief per bet with appetite, expected result, confidence and a stop rule | Yes |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `table`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

Label the commitment level. Name outcomes in the title of each bet. Five bets is the ceiling for most teams.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Source: bet briefs approved 6 Oct 2026."

## Titles

- Good: "Five bets for Q4. Three are promises, one is an experiment and one is a probe." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Next bets" (a label; it claims nothing).
