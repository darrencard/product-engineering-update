# Slide 7: Customer & market signal

Act: Discovery. Audiences: Leadership.

## The question it answers

What are customers and competitors telling us?

## Required data

Win and loss reasons with counts, support themes, call notes.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| CRM | Win and loss reasons with counts | Yes |
| Support desk | Support themes | Yes |
| Research and call notes | Call notes | No |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `tiles`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

Counts, never anecdotes. Say what each signal changes on the roadmap.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Sources: CRM win and loss notes on 64 closed deals, support desk. Q3 FY26."

## Titles

- Good: "Buyers choose us on agent accuracy and leave over a missing integration." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Customer & market signal" (a label; it claims nothing).

## Reading
