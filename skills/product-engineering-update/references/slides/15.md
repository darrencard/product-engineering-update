# Slide 15: Stopped & won't do

Act: Next investments. Audiences: Leadership, Staff.

## The question it answers

What did we kill or decline, and what did that free?

## Required data

A decision log. If none exists, start one this period.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Documents | A decision log | Yes |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `cards`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

An empty slide is a finding. Say what each stop cost and what it paid for.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Source: decision log, Q3 FY26."

## Titles

- Good: "We stopped three things this quarter to fund agent quality." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Stopped & won't do" (a label; it claims nothing).
