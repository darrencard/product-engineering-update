# Slide 12: AI in the product

Act: Measurement. Audiences: Leadership, Board.

## The question it answers

Is our AI good, used and profitable?

## Required data

A versioned eval set, production traces, cost per request, AI revenue and cost of revenue from Finance.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Evals and traces | A versioned eval set, production traces and cost per request | Yes |
| Finance actuals | AI revenue and cost of revenue | Yes |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `tiles`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

Show AI margin on its own line with a dated path to your target. Name the top failure modes with counts. When a number is missing, show a "data needed" card with an owner and a date.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Sources: eval harness v14, production traces, provider billing, Finance. Q3 FY26."

## Titles

- Good: "The agent passes its quality bar and costs 20% less per task. Margin is 3 points short." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "AI in the product" (a label; it claims nothing).

## Reading

- [ICONIQ, State of AI 2026](https://www.iconiq.com/growth/reports/state-of-ai-2026) (Jul 2026, survey of about 300 executives). AI product gross margin averaged 45% in 2025. The 2026 and 2027 figures are projections.
- [Husain and Shankar, error analysis](https://hamel.dev/notes/llm/ai-product-engineering/evals-error-analysis.html) (Jun 2026). Read failures first, then write checks.
- [Kyle Poyar, State of B2B Monetization 2025](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2025-state-of-b2b-monetization) (240 companies). Seat pricing fell from 21% to 15% and hybrid rose from 27% to 41%.
- [Product School, AI-native](https://productschool.com/blog/artificial-intelligence/ai-native) (Feb 2026). The remove-it test, and evals as a condition for shipping.
