# Slide 18: Decisions & asks

Act: Next investments. Audiences: Leadership, Board.

## The question it answers

What do you need from the people in the room?

## Required data

For each decision, the options, the cost of each, your recommendation and the deadline.

| Source | What the slide reads | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Intake interview | For each decision, the options, the cost of each, your recommendation and the deadline | Yes |

## Minimum evidence to render

At least one of these blocks, filled from computed data: `cards`.
If the data is missing, put a `data_needed` block in its place with an owner and a due date. Never fill the gap with prose.

## The rule

Brief the CEO and chair on any miss before the meeting. If there is nothing to decide, say so and end early.

## Time window

State the window on the slide's `asof` line. In the example: "Decisions due at the meeting on 4 Nov 2026."

## Titles

- Good: "We need two decisions today and one introduction." (a claim someone can check).
- Bad: "Decisions & asks" (a label; it claims nothing).

## Reading

- [First Round Review, Board Meetings 101](https://review.firstround.com/board-meetings-101-for-early-stage-founders/) (Aug 2026). "Thoughts?" is a weak ask. Say whether you want advice, pressure-testing, introductions or a formal decision.
- [Melissa Perri](https://productinstitute.kit.com/posts/the-bottleneck-isn-t-building-it-s-deciding) (2026). Deciding is now the constraint, so measure product work by decisions changed.
