Kestrel Labs · Product & Engineering update · 2026-Q3 (1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026)

The agent is clearing more loads, but revenue is growing slowly, activation is behind and inference costs are over plan

From the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Sources: PostHog, Stripe, CRM, Linear, Finance close, HR system, Incident tool, GitHub, Support desk, Developer survey

Summary
  1. The north star beat its target. The agent cleared 23,100 loads a week with no manual touch, against a target of 22K. That is up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers (6,120) also beat target.
  2. Revenue is not growing at the rate the $50M ARR objective needs. ARR grew from $10.38M to $10.91M (+5.1%). Net revenue retention was 96%. Four accounts churned, taking $1.11M of ARR, and all four had raised onboarding tickets.
  3. Activation is the input furthest from target. It reached 41% against a target of 50%. Guided activation shipped, and its live test will decide whether we continue it. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship.
  4. Missing TMS integrations are our largest loss reason, and no current bet addresses them. 11 of 17 lost deals cited "No TMS integration". 30 of 55 support tickets were integration errors.
  5. Inference cost $612K against a plan of $480K (+28%). Most of that spend goes to a single model provider, which is already on the risk register.
  6. We ship faster, but quality has slipped. We now deploy 4 times a week and lead time is down to 38h. The change failure rate rose to 7.7% (limit: under 5%) and review time rose to 13h (limit: under 10h). The VP Engineering owns both and will report back in Q4.
  7. One decision for you: approve a 10-customer per-load pricing pilot. We lost 6 deals on price this quarter.

6 · Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot.

Options: pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers. Per-load pricing is part of our strategy ("We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load"), and 6 of 17 lost deals this quarter cited price.

Decision deadline: 4 Nov 2026.

Strategy, for reference

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
Policy: We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
Actions: Raise agent task success to 90% (not yet measurable) · First automated load inside 14 days (41% of accounts in Q3; target 50%).
Non-goals: Build a carrier marketplace · Ship on-premise.
