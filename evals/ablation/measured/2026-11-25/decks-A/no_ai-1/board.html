What is working
  1. Customers buy the strategy. Every one of the 22 deals we won cited "agent clears exceptions".
  2. Multi-stop reroutes and the dispatcher inbox shipped, and both usage targets were beaten: 23,100 loads a week and 6,120 weekly active dispatchers. In total, 6 of 7 commitments shipped.
  3. Engineering ships faster: 4 deploys a week (up from 3) and a 38h lead time (down from 52h). R&D spend was 1% under plan.
What is not working, and what we are doing
  1. Retention and activation. All four churned accounts had raised onboarding tickets. Guided activation shipped and is in a live test, which it must pass to continue. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped.
  2. Integrations. 11 of 17 lost deals cited "No TMS integration", and 55% of support tickets were integration errors. A proposal will go to Q4 planning.
  3. Inference cost and concentration. Inference ran $612K against a $480K plan (+28%), mostly with one model provider. A routing layer across 2 providers is due Dec 2026.
  4. Release quality. The change failure rate rose to 7.7% (limit: under 5%). The VP Engineering owns this and will report back in Q4.

Decision requested

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter).

Per-load pricing is our stated strategy, and 6 of 17 lost deals this quarter cited price. Deadline: 4 Nov 2026.
