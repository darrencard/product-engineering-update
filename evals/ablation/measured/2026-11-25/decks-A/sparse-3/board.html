Three things to know
  1. Investment leaned toward the AI product. Dispatch Agent accounted for 31% of completed work and kept a steady pace all quarter. Core Dispatch improvement and growth took 26%, platform & scale 21%, and keep-the-lights-on 22%.
  2. Output fell from 53 items in July to 10 in September. Core Dispatch and Platform closed almost nothing in September. The data doesn't show why. It could be a real slowdown, larger work still in progress, or a reporting gap, and it needs an explanation before the Q4 plan is locked.
  3. We can't yet report outcomes. There are no agreed objectives, north-star metric, target investment mix or Dispatch Agent quality results yet. The VP Engineering will close these gaps in Q4 so the next update can report results, not just activity.
