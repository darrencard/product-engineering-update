Summary
  1. We completed 100 items in 2026-Q3. 31% were new capabilities, all on Dispatch Agent. Another 26% improved or grew Core Dispatch, 21% went to platform & scale, and 22% was keep-the-lights-on.
  2. Completions fell from 53 in July to 37 in August and 10 in September. Core Dispatch closed nothing after 21 Aug 2026. Platform closed 1 item in September. Only Dispatch Agent kept a steady pace (9 in September). The export has no in-progress work, so we can't yet tell whether this is a real slowdown, work in flight, or a data issue. Needs explanation
  3. We can report activity but not outcomes. The intake has no objectives, north-star metric, investment plan, eval results, risks or decisions. The VP Engineering owns closing these gaps by Q4. Data gap

Method & data notes

  • Source: one Linear export (100 rows, IDs unique). All rows are status Done, all completed between 1 Jul 2026 and 26 Sep 2026, all estimate = 1.
  • Work type, product line, team and objective are taken as-is from the export. Months are calendar months of completed_at.
  • Item titles are generic placeholders (for example, “New capabilities item 4”), so this update can't name specific features that shipped.
  • No figures were estimated or filled in from outside these files. Anything not in the export or intake appears as a gap.
  • Report prepared 25 Nov 2026 with data as of 30 Sep 2026. Anything after that date is not reflected.
