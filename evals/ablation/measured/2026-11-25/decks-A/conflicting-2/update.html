Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering Update

2026-Q3 (1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026). Data as of 30 Sep 2026. Prepared by the CPTO for the leadership team.

Company objective: Grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). All three product lines (Dispatch Agent, Core Dispatch, Platform) roll up to it.

Summary

  • ARR is $10.1M, $0.9M (8%) short of the $11.0M plan, per the finance close. Stripe's account-level ARR for September adds up to $10.91M. The two haven't been reconciled yet, so this report uses the finance number.
  • Net revenue retention was 96% (Stripe, June to September). Four accounts churned, worth $1.11M, and expansion of $0.74M didn't cover them. All four churned accounts had filed onboarding tickets.
  • We can't yet say whether we hit the north star. PostHog shows 23,100 loads cleared per week, above the 22K target. The warehouse shows 21,400, below it. Either way, it grew from Q2's 18,400.
  • Activation is 41% against a 50% target, up 2 points even though Guided activation shipped. Weekly active dispatchers (6,120) beat the 6,000 target.
  • Agent task success rose from 82% to 86% (eval set v14), and cost per task fell 20% to $0.33. But inference spend was 27% over plan ($612K vs $480K).
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments. Release checklist shipped, but change failure rate rose to 7.7%, outside our under-5% range. PR review time (13h) is also outside range.
  • Missing TMS integration is our top reason for losing deals (11 of 17 losses). It's also the top support theme (30 of 55 tickets). Nothing in the Q4 bets addresses it.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot.

Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Evidence: Price was the reason for 6 of the 17 lost deals this quarter (CRM). Our stated strategy is to win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load. Today's pricing doesn't match that.

Deadline passed The intake set a decision deadline of 4 Nov 2026, which was before this report was compiled (24 Nov 2026). If the decision has already been made, record the outcome. If not, the leadership team needs to set a new date.

  • Period filter: 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026. Q2 (1 Apr to 30 Jun) is used only for comparison. Records outside both quarters were excluded (one customer request, R-999).
