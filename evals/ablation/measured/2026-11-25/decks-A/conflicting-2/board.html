Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering, 2026-Q3

Board summary. Quarter ended 30 Sep 2026. Prepared by the CPTO. The full leadership update has sources and detail.

Headline

  • ARR is $10.1M, 8% below the $11.0M plan. Net revenue retention was 96%: four churned accounts ($1.11M) outweighed $0.74M of expansion.
  • The product is working where customers adopt it. Every won deal cites the agent clearing exceptions. Agent task success rose from 82% to 86%, and cost per task fell 20%.
  • Our top problems are integration and onboarding. Missing TMS integration lost 11 of 17 deals, and all four churned accounts had raised onboarding issues. Activation is 41% against a 50% target.

Decision

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter). Recommendation: run the pilot. Price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals, and per-load pricing is core to the strategy.

Deadline passed The intake deadline was 4 Nov 2026. Confirm the outcome, or set a new date.

Next quarter

  • Multi-stop reroutes (6 weeks, 5 engineers; high confidence): task success from 86% to 90%. We stop if evals gain under 2 points by week 3.
  • Guided activation (4 weeks, 3 engineers; experiment): activation from 41% to 50%. We stop if there's no lift in a 2-week live test.
  • Stopped: carrier marketplace (it didn't serve the strategy).

Risks

  • One model provider carries most inference spend (medium likelihood, high impact). Mitigation: a routing layer across 2 providers, due Dec 2026.
  • Churn is outpacing expansion, and integration isn't covered by the current bets.
  • Delivery quality slipped. Change failure rate is 7.7% (range under 5%), even though deploy frequency rose. The VP Engineering owns the fix, due Q4.
