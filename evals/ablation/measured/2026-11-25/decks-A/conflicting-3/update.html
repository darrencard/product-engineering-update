Product & Engineering update, 2026-Q3

Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · data as of 30 Sep 2026 · from the CPTO · compiled 25 Nov 2026 from the system exports listed in the appendix

Summary

  • The agent is getting better and cheaper. Dispatch Agent eval task success rose from 82% to 86% (v13 → v14), and cost per task fell 20%, from $0.41 to $0.33. That's still short of the 90% the Multi-stop reroutes bet aimed for.
  • I can't say whether we hit the north star. PostHog puts loads cleared per week at 23,100, above the 22K target. The warehouse puts it at 21,400, below it. Until the two agree, I'm reporting it as unresolved, not as a hit.
  • ARR is behind plan, and the sources disagree. The finance close shows ARR of $10.1M against an $11.0M plan. Adding up the Stripe accounts gives $10.9M. Revenue retention from Stripe was 96% net and 89% gross for the quarter. 4 accounts churned ($1.11M).
  • Activation barely moved: 41% against a 50% target, up from 39%. Guided activation shipped, but the quarter-level data shows none of the lift it was meant to deliver.
  • Integrations are the biggest gap, and nothing in the plan addresses them. 11 of 17 lost deals cite "No TMS integration", and 30 of 55 support tickets are integration errors.
  • Delivery is mixed. We deploy more often and restore faster. Change failure rate (7.7%) and review time (13h) got worse and are outside their ranges.
  • Overrun to watch: inference spend was $612K against a $480K plan (+27.5%), and one model provider carries most of it.

Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter. Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Evidence from this quarter: 6 of 17 lost deals cite price. The strategy commits to per-load pricing ("priced per load"), and this pilot is the first test of it.

Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. That date is earlier than this document's compile date (25 Nov 2026), and the source files don't say whether the decision was made. If it was, record the outcome here. If it wasn't, it's overdue.

Strategy we're executing

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle. Policy: we win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.

Actions: raise agent task success to 90% · get each account its first automated load inside 14 days. Not doing: a carrier marketplace (stopped this quarter because it didn't serve the strategy) · on-premise.

All three product lines serve one company objective, Grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). Dispatch Agent is in Expand. Core Dispatch and Platform are in Sustain.

Appendix: sources and method

  • Intake interview with the CPTO: strategy, targets, bets, commitments, risks, decision.
  • PostHog (analytics): north star, activation, weekly active dispatchers. Warehouse: second north-star figure (select avg(loads) from ns_weekly).
  • Stripe (billing): ARR per account for Jun and Sep 2026. Retention is calculated on accounts present in June. Finance close: plan vs actual for ARR, revenue, R&D lines and inference.
  • Eval harness: Dispatch Agent v13 and v14, 500 cases each. The intake names v14 as the current eval set.
  • Linear (tracker): 100 completed Q3 items. Allocation = share of item count. Linear (customer requests): 8 requests. Two (R-007, R-999, both "KES-388 Carrier rating export") have no link, and R-999 was created 2 May 2026, before this period.
  • GitHub: Q2 = Apr–Jun, Q3 = Jul–Sep, split by deploy date and by merge date for PRs. Deploys per week = deploys ÷ 13 weeks. Medians throughout.
  • Incident tool, support desk (55 tickets), CRM (39 deals), HR system, tool admin exports, developer survey.
