Decision: approve a per-load pricing pilot

Pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter. Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers. Price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals, and per-load pricing is central to our strategy.

Requested deadline: 4 Nov 2026. That date has passed, and the source files don't record an outcome. Please confirm whether the decision was made.

What went well

  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments, including Multi-stop reroutes and Guided activation, the quarter's two bets.
  • The agent got better and cheaper: eval task success rose from 82% to 86%, and cost per task fell 20%.
  • Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120, above the 6,000 target. Win rate was 56%, and every win cited the agent clearing exceptions.
  • We deployed 4 times a week, up from 3. R&D spend was 1% under plan at $4.2M.

What needs attention

  • ARR is $0.9M short of plan. 4 churned accounts took $1.11M. All 4 had raised onboarding tickets. Self-serve carrier onboarding, the one commitment we missed, targeted that area.
  • Integrations: 11 of 17 lost deals cite no TMS integration, and integration errors are 55% of support tickets. No current bet or commitment covers integrations, and no one owns the problem yet. Leadership needs to decide whether to fund integration work.
  • Inference cost was 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K), and most of it runs through one model provider. A routing layer across 2 providers is due Dec 2026 (owner: Platform lead).
  • Change quality slipped: change failure rate is 7.7%, against a range of under 5%. The VP Engineering owns the fix, due Q4.

Data caveat

Our systems give two different values for the north star (PostHog 23,100, warehouse 21,400) and two for ARR (finance $10.1M, Stripe account sum $10.9M). This update uses finance for ARR and doesn't claim the north-star target. The VP Engineering will reconcile both sources in Q4. Full detail is in the leadership update.
