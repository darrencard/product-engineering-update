Quarterly update: 2026-Q3

Period 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared for the leadership team by the CPTO · Company objective: grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO)

Summary

The Dispatch Agent strategy is working where it counts most. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose 26%, and every won deal cited the agent. Two things are off track: activation is well short of target, and change quality slipped while we sped up. Two numbers conflict between systems and are flagged below.

  • North star beat target, with a caveat. Loads cleared per week reached 23,100 against a 22K target, up from 18,400 in Q2. That figure is from PostHog, the source of truth. The warehouse reports 21,400, which would be a miss. The VP Engineering owns reconciling the two in Q4.
  • ARR is just under plan. Billing (source of truth) totals $10.91M against an $11.0M plan. Net revenue retention is 96%, because four churned accounts ($1.11M) outweighed expansion. The finance close reports $10.1M, and that $0.81M gap is unexplained.
  • Activation is the biggest miss. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points. Guided activation shipped, but its lift is not visible in Q3 data. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship.
  • We ship faster, but break more. We deploy 4 times a week (up from 3) and lead time fell from 52h to 38h. But change failure rate is 7.7% (range: under 5%) and PR review time is 13h (range: under 10h).
  • Inference spend ran 28% over plan ($612K against $480K), even though cost per successful task fell 20%. Volume grew faster than unit cost fell.
  • Integration is the largest unaddressed signal. 11 of 17 lost deals cited "No TMS integration," and 30 of 55 support tickets are integration errors. No current bet addresses this.

Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot

OptionsPilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
RecommendationRun the pilot with 10 customers. Our policy is "priced per load," and price was the second most common loss reason (6 of 17 lost deals).

  • Integration errors: 30 (A001–A030)
