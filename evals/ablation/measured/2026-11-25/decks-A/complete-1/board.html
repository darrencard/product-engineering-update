Delivery

We ship more often (4 deploys a week, up from 3) and changes reach production faster (38h, down from 52h). Reliability slipped: 7.7% of changes failed, against a target of under 5%, and code review takes 13h, against under 10h. The VP Engineering owns closing both gaps in Q4. Recovery from incidents stayed fast, at 41 minutes.

Focus for Q4

  • Get activation from 41% to 50%. That means deciding whether to stop or continue guided activation, and shipping self-serve carrier onboarding.

  • Bring change failure rate and review time back into range.
  • Bring inference cost back to plan by finishing the routing layer.
