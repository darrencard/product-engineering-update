Product & Engineering update — 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · from the CPTO · quarter 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · data as of 30 Sep 2026

Summary

  • The North Star beat its target. 23,100 loads a week were cleared with no manual touch, against a target of 22,000. That's up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers also beat target, at 6,120 against 6,000.
  • Activation is the miss. It's at 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points from Q2. Guided activation shipped, but activation is still at the bet's 41% starting point. Self-serve carrier onboarding, the other activation commitment, didn't ship.
  • Integrations are a gap with no plan behind them. “No TMS integration” is the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals. Integration errors are the top support theme: 30 of 55 tickets, raised by 30 of 40 accounts. No bet or commitment this quarter addresses it.
  • Inference cost $612K against a $480K plan (+28%). It took 5.9% of revenue against 4.8% planned. That's despite the cost of each eval task falling 20%. One provider carries most of this spend, and the routing layer that mitigates that is due in Dec 2026.
  • We ship faster but less safely. Deploys rose to 4.0 a week and lead time fell to 38h, both inside range. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (range: under 5%) and PR review time to 13h (range: under 10h). The VP Engineering owns closing both gaps in Q4.
  • One decision for leadership: whether to approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers. The deadline was 4 Nov 2026 (see below).

Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter. Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why now: the strategy says we win mid-market brokers “priced per load”, but we still don't price that way. Price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals this quarter (35%). A quarter's delay means another quarter of losses to price with no evidence either way.

Timing: the deadline in the intake was 4 Nov 2026. This report was compiled on 24 Nov 2026, so if the decision hasn't been made yet, it's overdue.

Strategy, for reference

Diagnosis: dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.

Guiding policy: we win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.

Actions: raise agent task success to 90% (now 86%) · get customers to their first automated load inside 14 days (activation is now 41% against 50%).

Product lines: Dispatch Agent (Expand) · Core Dispatch (Sustain) · Platform (Sustain).

About the data

  • Sources: PostHog, Stripe, CRM, Linear (tracker and customer requests), eval harness, finance close, HR system, incident tool, GitHub, support desk, developer survey, and admin exports from the AI tools.
  • The period is 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026. Q2 comparisons use the same exports. The ARR bridge compares June and September billing snapshots.
  • Lead time is measured from PR opened to merged, because the export doesn't link PRs to deploys. Change failure rate is failed deploys divided by all deploys.
  • The CRM and support exports have no dates, so they're assumed to cover Q3.
  • The billing ARR doesn't reconcile with finance revenue (see section 2).
  • Review times and incident durations are uniform within each quarter (see section 6).
