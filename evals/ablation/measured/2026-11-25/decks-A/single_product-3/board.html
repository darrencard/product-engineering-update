What the board should know

  • The strategy is working. Every deal we won cited the same reason: the agent clears exceptions. Usage beat target, and we shipped 6 of 7 commitments.
  • Activation is the main gap. It's 9 points short. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped. All 4 accounts that churned ($1.1M ARR) had raised an onboarding ticket in Q3. Next quarter we'll test Guided activation, aiming to lift activation from 41% to 50%. If it shows no lift in a 2-week test, we'll stop it.
  • We're losing deals to missing TMS integrations. That caused 11 of our 17 losses, and integration errors make up 55% of support tickets. None of our current bets targets integration.
  • Spend: R&D is on plan at $4.2M (41% of revenue). Inference spend was 28% over plan at $612K. That's because loads grew 26%. Cost per task fell 20%, so unit economics improved.
  • Engineering: We ship more often and faster, but the change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%). The VP Engineering owns bringing it back under target in Q4.
