Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering update, Q3 2026

Period: 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · From the CPTO · Audience: leadership team

Summary

  • We beat the north-star target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100 a week against a 22,000 target, up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120, which also beats the 6,000 target.
  • Activation is the biggest miss. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up just 2 points. Guided activation shipped, but its effect doesn't show up yet. Self-serve carrier onboarding didn't ship.
  • The lack of a TMS integration is costing deals, and none of our current bets addresses it. It explains 11 of the 17 deals we lost. Integration errors also make up 30 of the 55 support tickets.
  • Inference spend came in 28% over plan ($612K against $480K). That's because volume grew: cost per agent task fell from $0.41 to $0.33. Most of that spend still goes through one model provider.
  • Mixed results on delivery. We're shipping faster: 4 deploys a week, and a PR now takes 38h from open to merge, down from 52h. But the change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%), and PR review time rose to 13h (target under 10h). The VP Engineering owns closing both gaps in Q4.
  • Keep-the-lights-on work took 22% of delivered items against a 15% plan. That squeezed new capabilities to 31%, against a 35% plan.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot.

Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.
Deadline: 4 Nov 2026

Why now: our policy is to win mid-market brokers by pricing per load. We lost 6 of 17 deals on price.

This update was compiled on 25 Nov 2026, after the 4 Nov deadline. The source files don't say whether a decision was made, so confirm before acting on this ask.

Strategy (unchanged)

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
Policy: We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
Actions: Raise agent task success to 90%, and get each new account to its first automated load within 14 days.
Not doing: Building a carrier marketplace (stopped this quarter because it didn't serve the strategy), or shipping on-premise.

One product line, Dispatch Agent, is in the Expand stage. It serves the company objective Grow ARR to $50M, which the CEO owns.

Revenue (billing cohort)

The Stripe export covers 46 accounts. Across them, ARR rose from $10.38M to $10.91M (+5.1%):

  • New: 6 accounts at $150K each, $0.90M in total.
  • Expansion: 15 accounts, each up about 20%, $0.74M in total.
  • Churn: 4 accounts, $1.11M in total.

For accounts that existed at the start of the quarter, net revenue retention was 96% and gross retention was 89%. Q3 revenue was $10.3M against a $10.0M plan.

All 4 churned accounts (A037–A040) had raised an onboarding ticket in Q3. The sample is too small to show cause, but it fits the activation gap.

Can't state company-wide ARR against the $50M objective from these files. See Data notes.

Data notes

  • The ARR figures don't add up. The Stripe export totals $10.9M ARR across 42 active accounts. But Q3 revenue of $10.3M implies roughly $41M a year. The export is probably a partial cohort, so this update doesn't state progress toward $50M ARR.
  • Lead time stops at merge, not deploy. PRs aren't linked to deploys in the GitHub export. The VP Engineering will add that link in Q4.
  • Effort shares are item counts. Every tracker item has an estimate of 1. The tracker titles are generic, so we can't match items to individual commitments.
  • The bets' starting points are end-of-Q3 values (86% task success, 41% activation). Yet both bets also appear as commitments shipped in Q3. The files don't say which work is still to come.
  • Two customer requests have no link. R-007 and R-999 (Carrier rating export) aren't linked to any work. R-999 is from May 2026, outside Q3, so it's left out of the Q3 count.
  • Data from 13 exports across 12 systems: Tool admin exports, PostHog, Stripe, CRM, Linear, eval harness, Finance close, HR system, Incident tool, GitHub, Support desk and the Developer survey.
