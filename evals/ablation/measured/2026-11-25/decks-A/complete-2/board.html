Decision for the board

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers. The alternative is to wait a quarter.

We recommend the pilot. Per-load pricing is our strategy, and price was the reason for 6 of our 17 lost deals this quarter.

The deadline was 4 Nov 2026. This summary is going out on 24 Nov 2026, so the decision is overdue if it hasn't already been made.

What went well

  • We beat the North Star target: 23,100 loads a week were cleared with no manual touch. Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120, against a target of 6,000.
  • The agent got better and cheaper: task success rose from 82% to 86%, and cost per task fell 20%.
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments. We stopped the carrier marketplace because it didn't serve the strategy.
  • We now ship faster: 4 deploys a week, up from 3, with lead time down to 38h from 52h. R&D spend was on plan at $4.20M (41% of revenue).

What needs attention

  • Activation is at 41% against a 50% target. Guided activation shipped and is now being tested. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped.
  • Inference spend ran 28% over plan ($612K against $480K), and most of it goes to one model provider. A routing layer across 2 providers is due in Dec 2026.
  • More of what we ship is failing: change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%), and review time rose to 13h (target under 10h).
  • Integration is the gap in our plan. 11 of 17 lost deals cited no TMS integration, and integration errors made up 55% of support tickets. The VP Engineering owns a proposal, due in Q4.
  • Churn outpaced expansion in the accounts covered by the billing export: we lost $1.11M of ARR to churn against $0.74M of expansion, for 96% net revenue retention. That export doesn't match the finance close, so we can't yet report progress toward the $50M ARR goal. Reconciling the two is a Q4 action.
