Product & Engineering update: Q3 2026

Kestrel Labs · from the CPTO · Covers 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Compared with Q2 2026 unless stated

Summary

  • The North Star is ahead of target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100 a week against a 22K target, up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers (6,120) also beat target.
  • Activation is the miss. It reached 41%, up from 39% but well short of the 50% target. Guided activation shipped, but we don't have a post-launch reading yet. Self-serve carrier onboarding, also aimed at activation, did not ship.
  • The agent got better and cheaper. Task success on eval set v14 rose from 82% to 86%, still short of our 90% goal. Cost per task fell 20%, from $0.41 to $0.33.
  • Inference spend came in 28% over plan ($612K against $480K). Most of that spend also sits with one model provider. Both of these need attention.
  • Delivery got faster but less safe. We now ship 4 deploys a week, up from 3, and lead time is down to 38h from 52h. But change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%), and review time rose to 13h (target under 10h). Both moved even though the release checklist shipped.
  • Customers are pointing at a gap the plan doesn't cover. 11 of 17 lost deals cited no TMS integration, and 30 of 55 support tickets were integration errors. Neither our Q3 work nor our current bets address this.
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments. R&D spend was on plan at $4.20M, against $4.25M.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers. Per-load pricing is our stated strategy. Price was also the second most common reason we lost deals (6 of 17 losses).

Decide by: 4 Nov 2026. This update is going out on 24 Nov 2026, after that deadline. If the decision hasn't been made yet, it is overdue.

Strategy recap

DiagnosisDispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
PolicyWe win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
ActionsRaise agent task success to 90% (now 86%) · First automated load inside 14 days (activation 41%, target 50%)
Non-goalsBuild a carrier marketplace (stopped this quarter) · Ship on-premise
ObjectiveGrow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). All three product lines serve it: Dispatch Agent (Expand), Core Dispatch (Sustain), Platform (Sustain).

The diagnosis is holding up. All 22 won deals gave the same reason: "Agent clears exceptions".
