What the board should know

  • The core product works. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose 26% and beat target. Every won deal cited the same reason: the agent clears exceptions.
  • Onboarding is the weak point. Activation is 9 points below target. Self-serve carrier onboarding, one of our seven commitments, did not ship. All 4 accounts that churned had raised onboarding tickets. In the billing export, net revenue retention was 96%.
  • The missing TMS integration is costing deals. It was the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals and drove 30 of 55 support tickets. It is not in the current plan, and management will decide on it in Q4 planning.
  • Inference costs ran over plan. Inference spend was $612K against a $480K plan (+28%) because volume grew faster than unit cost fell. Cost per agent task is down 20%. Most of the spend goes to one model provider, and a routing layer across two providers is due in Dec 2026.
  • Engineering ships faster, with more failures. Deploys rose to 4 a week and lead time fell to 38h. The change failure rate rose to 7.7%, above our 5% limit. Recovery time from incidents stays well within range at 41 minutes.
  • R&D spend is on plan. Q3 spend was $4.20M against a $4.25M plan. Engineering headcount is 44 against a plan of 46.

Caveat on ARR

We are not reporting progress toward the $50M ARR objective this quarter. The billing export ($10.9M ARR across 42 accounts) does not reconcile with finance revenue ($10.3M for the quarter). The VP Engineering will reconcile the two in Q4.
