Summary

  1. Dispatch Agent, our AI feature, was the main focus. It got 31% of completed work and kept a steady pace all quarter. Platform and upkeep took 43%, improving the core product 18%, and growth 8%.
  2. Overall output fell across the quarter, and we’re finding out why. Completions dropped from 53 in July to 10 in September. By September only Dispatch Agent work was still closing. The data can’t yet tell us whether this reflects reassigned people, open work not yet finished, or a tracking issue. That’s the first thing to check.
  3. We can’t yet report outcomes. This quarter’s data covers delivery activity only. We don’t yet have objectives with targets, a north-star metric, Dispatch Agent quality results, or a risk register. The VP Engineering owns putting them in place, due Q4.

No decisions are requested from the board this quarter. Shares are by issue count; every issue carries the same estimate, so they don’t measure effort. The full leadership update has the detail.
