Q3 2026: 100 issues shipped, but the monthly count fell from 53 in July to 10 in September

Period 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30 · Data as of 2026-09-30 · From the CPTO · Only source: Linear issue tracker

Summary

  1. 100 issues were completed in Q3. 31 (31%) were new capabilities, all on Dispatch Agent. 43 (43%) went to keep-the-lights-on and platform work, 18 (18%) to improving the core product, and 8 (8%) to growth.
  2. Completions fell every month: 53 in July, 37 in August, 10 in September. By September only Dispatch Agent feature work was still closing (9 of 10 issues). The last Growth issue closed Jul 22, the last Improve-the-core issue Aug 21, and the last Platform issue Aug 30. We don’t yet know whether this is a real slowdown, a shift of people onto Dispatch Agent, or work finished but not marked Done. This is the main open question.
  3. This update reports activity, not outcomes. The intake had no objectives, north-star metric, strategy, plan, risks, or commitments, and the tracker is the only connected system. So this update can’t say whether Q3 moved the business. The VP Engineering owns closing these gaps in Q4.

Every work type except new capabilities stopped closing issues before the quarter ended:

  • Growth closed 8 issues, all in July. The last one was Jul 22.
  • Improve the core closed nothing after Aug 21.
  • Platform & scale closed nothing after Aug 30. Keep the lights on closed nothing after Sep 2.
  • New capabilities (Dispatch Agent) kept a steady pace all quarter: 12 / 10 / 9 issues.

The export has only Done issues, with no in-progress, backlog, or cancelled ones. That means we can’t tell whether the other teams stopped, moved to Dispatch Agent, or are carrying open work. The KTLO drop needs a check of its own: either upkeep demand fell or upkeep is no longer being tracked.

Also worth checking: 6 issues are dated Jul 1, the first day of the quarter, more than on any other day. One of them (NEW-31) is out of sequence with the rest of its series. That could mean a bulk close or carry-over from Q2, which would push the July count up.

Dispatch Agent (AI feature)

Dispatch Agent is the only AI feature listed at intake. It got all 31 new-capability issues in Q3 and was the only area still shipping in September. Eval set v14 is named, but no eval scores, regression results, or cost or latency figures were provided. We can show how much work went into it, but not whether its quality improved.

Next steps
