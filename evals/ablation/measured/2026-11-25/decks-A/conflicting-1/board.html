What the quarter showed

  • The strategy is working where it applies. All 22 deals we won gave "Agent clears exceptions" as the reason. Agent task success rose from 82% to 86% (the goal is 90%), and cost per task fell 20%.
  • Two things are holding growth back: activation, and missing TMS integrations. Integration accounted for 11 of 17 lost deals and was the top support theme. The one commitment we missed (self-serve carrier onboarding) was aimed at activation. We shipped 6 of 7 commitments overall.
  • Engineering ships faster, but quality has slipped. We deploy about 4 times a week, and lead time is down to 38h. Change failure rate has risen to 7.7%, though, against a target under 5%. The VP Engineering owns fixing this in Q4.
  • Spend: R&D came in at $4.20M against a $4.245M plan. Inference ran 28% over plan ($612K against $480K).

Decision: approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter). Pricing per load is part of our strategy, and price was the reason for 6 of the 17 deals we lost. I recommend the pilot.
