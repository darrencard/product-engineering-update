2026-Q3: the agent is winning deals, but activation, integrations and quality are holding growth back

From the CPTO · Quarter 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 24 Nov 2026

Our strategy is to win mid-market brokers by automating the exceptions that rules engines can't handle, priced per load. The Q3 data backs the diagnosis: every one of the 22 deals we won gave "Agent clears exceptions" as the reason. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose about 16–26% over Q2. ARR still finished behind plan, though, and the causes sit outside the agent.

  • North star: 21.4K–23.1K loads cleared per week, against a target of 22K. Our two sources sit on opposite sides of the target, so I can't yet say whether we hit it.
  • ARR: $10.1M against an $11.0M plan, per the finance close. Net revenue retention was 96%, and 4 accounts churned, taking $1.11M of ARR with them.
  • Activation is the weakest input: 41% against a 50% target. The one commitment we missed this quarter, self-serve carrier onboarding, was aimed at this number.
  • Integrations are the gap no plan covers: 11 of 17 lost deals cited "No TMS integration", and integration errors were the top support theme (30 of 55 tickets). None of our current bets addresses this.
  • We ship faster, but quality has slipped: lead time fell from 52h to 38h. Change failure rate rose to 7.7%, though, and PR review time to 13h. Both are now outside their target ranges.
  • Inference spend came in 28% over plan ($612K against $480K), even though cost per agent task fell 20%.

Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot. The options are to pilot with 10 customers or wait a quarter. I recommend running the pilot with 10 customers. Per-load pricing is part of our strategy, and price was the reason for 6 of the 17 deals we lost.

Deadline passed The deadline for this decision was 4 Nov 2026. These files don't record whether it was made. If it is still open, it should be settled now.

2. Strategy and bets

Diagnosis: dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle. Policy: win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load. Actions: raise agent task success to 90%, and get each customer to their first automated load inside 14 days. Non-goals: a carrier marketplace and on-premise deployment.
