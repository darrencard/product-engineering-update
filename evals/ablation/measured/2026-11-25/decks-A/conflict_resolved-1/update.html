Q3 2026 update for leadership

Period 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 24 Nov 2026 from the CPTO intake and system exports · Author: the CPTO

Summary

  • The agent is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose 26% to 23,100/week, above the 22K target (PostHog, our source of truth). Agent task success went from 82% to 86% and cost per task fell 20%. Every won deal (22 of 22) gave “agent clears exceptions” as the reason.
  • Revenue retention is the problem. Billing ARR grew 5% to $10.91M, just under the $11.0M plan. Four accounts churned, taking $1.11M. That is more than new business added ($0.90M) and more than expansion added ($0.74M). Quarterly net revenue retention was 96%.
  • Activation didn't move. It went from 39% to 41% against a 50% target. Guided activation shipped, but self-serve carrier onboarding did not.
  • Engineering shipped faster but less reliably. Deploys went from 3 to 4 a week and lead time from 52h to 38h. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (ceiling 5%) and PR review time to 13h (ceiling 10h), even though the release checklist shipped.
  • Inference cost $612K, 27.5% over plan. Volume grew faster than cost per task fell. Most of that spend goes to one model provider.
  • Integration is the biggest unaddressed signal. It was the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals and 30 of 55 support tickets. No current bet covers it.

Data notes and open reconciliations

  • ARR: two sources disagree. Stripe account-level ARR adds up to $10.91M. The finance close reports $10.1M. The intake names billing as the source of truth, so this update uses $10.91M. That puts ARR 0.8% short of the $11.0M plan; on finance's figure the shortfall would be 8%. This $0.81M gap needs reconciling.
  • Loads cleared: two sources disagree. PostHog reports 23,100 a week and the warehouse reports 21,400. The intake names PostHog as the source of truth, so the north star counts as hit. On the warehouse figure it would be a miss by 600. The warehouse query (select avg(loads) from ns_weekly) doesn't visibly apply the “no manual touch” filter, so the two figures may not measure the same thing.
  • Revenue line. Finance shows Q3 revenue of $10.3M against a $10.0M plan. A quarter's revenue at that level doesn't fit ARR of about $11M, so the line may be on a different basis, such as annualized or year to date. Because of that, this update doesn't use it for any ratio.
  • Churn definition. An account with a June ARR row and no September row is counted as churned.
  • Investment mix counts completed tracker items. Every item carries an estimate of 1, so the mix shows item counts, not effort.
  • Lead time is PR open to merge. The export doesn't link PRs to deploys.
