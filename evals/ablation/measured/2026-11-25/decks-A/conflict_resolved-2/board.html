What happened

  • The strategy is working where we've applied it. Every one of the 22 won deals cited the agent clearing exceptions. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose 26% and beat target. Cost per agent task fell 20%.
  • ARR was $10.91M, against an $11.0M plan. We added $0.90M from new accounts and $0.74M from expansion. We lost $1.11M when 4 accounts churned. Net revenue retention was 96% for the quarter.
  • Activation missed at 41% against 50%. Guided activation shipped but hasn't shown a lift yet, and self-serve carrier onboarding slipped.
  • Integration is the open gap. It was the top reason we lost deals (11 of 17) and the top support theme (30 of 55 tickets).
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments. R&D spend was $4.20M, on plan. Inference spend ran 27% over plan.

Decision: Approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options
Pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter
Recommendation
Run the pilot with 10 customers. This tests per-load pricing, which is central to the strategy. Price was the reason in 6 lost deals this quarter.
Deadline
4 Nov 2026 (passed). If it has not been decided yet, it is overdue

Risks

  • Model provider concentration (medium likelihood, high impact). One provider carries most inference spend. Mitigation: a routing layer across two providers, due Dec 2026.
  • Activation. If the guided-activation test shows no lift, the 50% target slips again.
  • Reliability. Change failure rate is 7.7% against a target under 5%. Time to restore is healthy at 41 minutes.

Q4 focus

  1. Lift activation toward 50%.
  2. Push agent task success toward 90%.
  3. Scope an integration bet.
  4. Bring change failure rate back under 5%.

Data caveats. The warehouse reports 21.4K loads/week, against 23.1K in PostHog. The finance close reports $10.10M ARR, against $10.91M in billing. We are reporting the system of record for each. Both gaps are being reconciled by the VP Engineering in Q4. The full leadership update has the details.
