Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: Pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why: the strategy says we are “priced per load”, and price is the second-biggest reason we lose: 6 of 17 lost deals in the CRM export cite price. A 10-customer pilot tests per-load pricing before we roll it out to everyone.

Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. That date has passed (this update was prepared 25 Nov 2026 from data as of 30 Sep). If the decision hasn’t been taken, we need a new date at this meeting.

What’s working

  • Every one of the 22 deals we won cites the agent clearing exceptions. The no-touch north star is 23.1K loads a week, above the 22K target.
  • Agent task success rose to 86% (the target is 90%), and cost per task fell 20%.
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments. We’re deploying 4.0 times a week, and R&D spend is 1.1% under plan.

What isn’t working

  • Churn: 4 accounts ($1.11M ARR) churned, and every one had raised onboarding tickets. Self-serve onboarding slipped, and activation is 41% against a 50% target.
  • Integration: “No TMS integration” is the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals. Integration errors affect accounts holding 77% of ARR. Nothing in the plan addresses this yet. The VP Engineering owns the plan, due in Q4.
  • Quality: change failure rate is 7.7% against a target under 5%.

Top risk

One model provider carries most inference spend. Inference is 27% over plan. Mitigation: routing layer across 2 providers, due Dec 2026.
