Kestrel Labs · Product & Engineering update · Q3 2026 (1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026)

The agent is winning deals, but churn from onboarding and a missing TMS integration are holding ARR back

From the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 25 Nov 2026

Summary
  1. The core bet is working. We cleared 23.1K loads a week with no manual touch, against a 22K target (up from 18.4K in Q2, +26%). All 22 won deals cite “agent clears exceptions”. The agent completes 86% of eval tasks (up from 82%), and each task costs 20% less.
  2. Net revenue retention was below 100%. ARR grew 5.1% to $10.91M, but 4 accounts churned and took $1.11M with them. Net revenue retention was 96% and gross revenue retention was 89%. Every churned account had raised an onboarding ticket. Self-serve carrier onboarding was the one commitment we didn’t ship, and activation is 41% against a 50% target.
  3. Nothing on the plan covers integration. 11 of 17 lost deals cite “No TMS integration”. Integration errors are the biggest support theme: 30 accounts holding 77% of ARR. No bet, commitment or investment line covers integration. The VP Engineering owns a plan, due in Q4.
  4. We’re shipping faster, but quality is slipping. We deploy 4.0 times a week, up from 3.0. Change failure rate rose from 5.1% to 7.7% (target under 5%), and median review time rose from 9h to 13h. Keeping the lights on took 22% of completed work against a 15% plan.
  5. Inference spend is 27% over plan ($612K against $480K). Most of it goes to one model provider, which is the main risk on our register. The mitigation is due in Dec 2026.
Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: Pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why: the strategy says we are “priced per load”, and price is the second-biggest reason we lose: 6 of 17 lost deals in the CRM export cite price. A 10-customer pilot tests per-load pricing before we roll it out to everyone.

Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. That date has passed (this update was prepared 25 Nov 2026 from data as of 30 Sep). If the decision hasn’t been taken, we need a new date at this meeting.
