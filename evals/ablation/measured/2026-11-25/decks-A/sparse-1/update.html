Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering update, 2026-Q3

From the CPTO · For leadership · Covers Jul 1 – Sep 30, 2026 · Data as of 2026-09-30

Summary

  1. We closed 100 issues across three product lines in Q3, and 78% of them were work other than keep-the-lights-on. The biggest share was 31% new capabilities, all on Dispatch Agent. Platform & scale took 21%, improving the core 18% and growth 8%. Keep-the-lights-on was 22%. These are ticket counts, not effort, because every issue is sized at 1.
  2. Closures fell through the quarter: 53 in July, 37 in August, 10 in September. In September, 9 of the 10 were Dispatch Agent. Core Dispatch closed nothing after Aug 21, and Platform nothing after Sep 2. Don't read this as a slowdown yet. The dates follow a mechanical 3-day pattern, so they may not reflect when work really shipped.
  3. This update can't tell you whether we're winning. We have no outcome metric, no objective progress, no Dispatch Agent eval results, no risks and no forward plan. Closing those gaps belongs to the VP Engineering, due in Q4. Until then this is an activity report, not a performance report.

Treat these dates with caution. In every stream, issues closed exactly 3 days apart starting July 1. The drop-off just reflects each stream running out of issues at that fixed rhythm. The likelier explanation is bulk-updated dates or a tracker workflow artifact, not a real change in delivery speed. We'll confirm before drawing any conclusion about Core Dispatch or Platform going quiet in September.

AI: Dispatch Agent

Dispatch Agent received all 31 new-capability issues this quarter (31% of everything closed). It was also the only product line still closing issues steadily in September. Its eval set is v14, but we have no scores, trend or pass threshold for it. Without those we can't say whether this work made the agent better, or whether it's safe to scale.
