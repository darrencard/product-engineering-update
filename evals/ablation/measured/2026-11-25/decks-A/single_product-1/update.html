Q3 2026: we hit the north star, but integrations are costing us deals and delivery quality slipped

Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · data as of 30 Sep 2026 · objective: grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO)

Summary
  • The north star beat target. Loads cleared per week with no manual touch averaged 23,100 against a target of 22K, up 25.5% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120 against a target of 6,000.
  • Activation is the miss. It reached 41% against a target of 50%. Guided activation shipped, but we have no reading taken after it shipped yet. Self-serve carrier onboarding was the only commitment that didn't ship (6 of 7 shipped).
  • Integrations are the biggest signal that no plan covers. "No TMS integration" was the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals, and "Integration errors" accounts for 30 of 55 support tickets. No current bet or commitment addresses it.
  • Delivery got faster but less safe. We now deploy 4 times a week (up from 3). But change failure rate rose to 7.7% (the target is under 5%) and review time rose to 13h (the target is under 10h). The release checklist shipped but hasn't reduced failures yet.
  • Inference spend was 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K) while revenue came in 3% over. This makes the risk of relying on one model provider more pressing.

Approve a per-load pricing pilot
Options

Recommendation
Run the pilot with 10 customers.
Why now
Our strategy is to be "priced per load." Price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals in the CRM export, so a pilot tests whether per-load pricing wins back that group.

Customer and market signal

Why deals were lost (17 of 39)

We won 22 of 39 deals (56%). Every won deal gave the same reason: "Agent clears exceptions."

Support tickets by theme (55)

Integration errors were reported by 30 accounts (A001–A030).

What this means: Customers buy because the agent clears exceptions, which confirms the strategy. They walk away when it doesn't connect to their TMS, and existing customers hit integration errors more than anything else. Integrations aren't a non-goal, but no Q3 bet or commitment addresses them. I suggest we decide in Q4 planning whether integrations should be a funded bet.

Strategy recap

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
Policy: We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
Actions: Raise agent task success to 90% (now 86%). Get the first automated load done inside 14 days (activation now 41%, target 50%).
