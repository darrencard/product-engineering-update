Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering update, Q3 2026

Period 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · From the CPTO · Built from 11 system exports. Sources and caveats are in the appendix.

Summary

  • We beat the north star, but activation stalled. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week against a 22K target, up 26% from Q2. Activation was 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points.
  • Onboarding is where we lose customers. All 4 accounts that churned ($1.11M ARR) had filed onboarding tickets. Self-serve carrier onboarding, the one commitment we missed, was aimed at this problem.
  • ARR is $10.91M, up 5.1% this quarter (net revenue retention 96%). New logos and expansion more than covered churn. We are still a long way from the $50M objective.
  • Two delivery measures are outside our target ranges. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%) even after the release checklist shipped. Review time rose to 13h (target under 10h).
  • Inference cost was $612K, 28% over plan. Most of it runs through one model provider, which is already on our risk list.
  • One decision is open: the per-load pricing pilot. Its 4 Nov deadline has passed. Price was the reason we lost 6 of 17 deals.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot.
Options: run the pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why: pricing per load is the core of our strategy (“we win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load”). In the CRM, 6 of 17 lost deals cite price.

Deadline passed The original deadline was 4 Nov 2026, and this update is being issued on 24 Nov 2026. If no decision has been made, we need a new date.

Diagnosis. Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can’t handle.

Policy. We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.

Objective. Grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). All three product lines serve it: Dispatch Agent (Expand), Core Dispatch (Sustain), Platform (Sustain).

  • First automated load inside 14 days (this is the activation metric) 41% vs 50%
Non-goals

  • Ship on-premise

Appendix: data gaps, caveats and sources

Gaps to close (owner: the VP Engineering, due Q4)

  • Agent task success isn't in any export. It is a strategy action and the success measure for the Multi-stop reroutes bet.
  • Cost per successful task isn't exported. It is the metric for Cheaper agent retries and the key to explaining the inference overrun.
  • Guided activation live-test readout is needed before we can apply the stop rule.
  • Lead time to production: PRs need to be linked to the deploys that shipped them.
  • Finance revenue vs Stripe ARR: finance reports Q3 revenue of $10.3M (plan $10.0M), while Stripe shows ARR of $10.9M. Quarterly revenue that close to annual run-rate doesn't add up. One of the two figures is defined differently or incomplete. Reconcile before this goes to the board.
  • CRM and support exports have no dates or values, so we can't scope them to Q3.
  • Request hygiene: R-007 (KES-388) has no Linear link. R-999 is dated before the period.

Other caveats

  • Linear items closed were front-loaded across the quarter (53 in Jul, 37 in Aug, 10 in Sep). That may reflect how items are closed rather than a drop in output. Worth checking before anyone reads it as a trend.
  • Retention figures cover one quarter, measured Jun → Sep, and are not annualized.
