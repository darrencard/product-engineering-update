Product & Engineering update: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 25 Nov 2026 · Author: the CPTO
Sources: Linear, GitHub, PostHog, Stripe, CRM, Support desk, Eval harness, Incident tool, Finance close, HR system, tool admin exports, developer survey.

Summary

  • North star hit. 23,100 loads per week cleared with no manual touch, against a 22K target. Up 25.5% from 18,400 in Q2.
  • Activation missed. 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points. Guided activation shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding, the other activation commitment, did not.
  • Delivery is faster but less stable. Deploys rose to 4 a week and lead time fell to 38h. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%) and PR review time rose to 13h (target under 10h). The VP Engineering owns both gaps, due Q4.
  • Inference is 28% over plan ($612K vs $480K), even though eval cost per task fell 20%. One model provider carries most of this spend.
  • Integrations are the biggest customer signal, and no current bet covers them. "No TMS integration" caused 11 of 17 lost deals, and "Integration errors" accounts for 30 of 55 support tickets.
  • The ARR sources disagree and need reconciling before anyone reports progress toward the $50M objective (see Revenue).

Sources: GitHub, Incident tool, developer survey, tool admin exports. Lead time here is PR opened to merged, because the export doesn't link commits to deploys. Each quarter is 13 weeks: 60 PRs merged in Q2 and 60 in Q3.
