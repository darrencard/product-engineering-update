What the board should know

  • The product thesis is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose 26% and beat target. All 22 deals won in Q3 cited the agent clearing exceptions.
  • Revenue growth is held back by churn and integration. ARR grew 5.1% to $10.91M, against an objective of $50M. Four accounts churned ($1.11M), which outweighed new logos ($0.90M). Missing TMS integration lost 11 of 17 deals and drove most support tickets. It is not in the current plan. The VP Engineering will bring a plan in Q4.
  • Activation missed. It reached 41% against 50%. Guided activation shipped, but activation rose only 2 points.
  • Spend is on plan, with one exception. R&D came in at $4.20M against a $4.25M plan. Inference ran 28% over plan ($612K) because volume grew, even though cost per task fell 20%. Most of that spend is with one provider, and a routing layer across two providers is due in Dec 2026.
  • Engineering shipped 6 of 7 commitments and is shipping faster. But the change failure rate rose to 7.7%, above the 5% target.

Decision

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter. Management recommends running the pilot. Six of 17 lost deals cited price.
The original deadline was 4 Nov 2026 and has passed. These files contain no record of a decision.

Key risk

One model provider carries most inference spend. Likelihood is medium and impact is high. The mitigation is a routing layer across 2 providers, owned by the Platform lead and due in Dec 2026.
