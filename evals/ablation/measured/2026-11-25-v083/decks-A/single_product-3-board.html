What happened

  • Delivery: 6 of 7 commitments shipped, including multi-stop reroutes and guided activation. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped.
  • Revenue: ARR grew from $10.38M to $10.91M. That was +$900K from 6 new logos and +$741K from expansion, offset by −$1.11M from 4 churned accounts. The objective is $50M ARR. The intake gives no target date for it.
  • Customers: we won 22 of 39 deals, all on exception automation. Of the 17 losses, 11 were for missing TMS integration and 6 were on price. No current bet or commitment targets integrations.
  • Spend: R&D was $4.20M, on plan and 41% of revenue. Inference was $612K, 28% over plan, mostly with one model provider. A routing layer across 2 providers is due Dec 2026.
  • Engineering health: deploy pace rose to 4.0 a week. Change failure rate (7.7%) is above our 5% limit. The release checklist meant to bring it down has shipped, but it has not worked yet.
  • Team: 53 of 56 planned, with 3 roles open.

Bets in flight (both shipped in Q3, outcomes still to show)

  • Multi-stop reroutes (promise): Task success 86% to 90%. We stop if: evals gain under 2 points by week 3.
  • Guided activation (experiment): Activation 41% to 50%. We stop if: no lift in a 2 week live test.
