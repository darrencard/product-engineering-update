Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering update, 2026-Q3

1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · data as of 30 Sep 2026 · prepared by the CPTO · for the leadership team

Summary

  • The north star beat target. Loads cleared per week averaged 23.1K a week, against a target of 22K (Q2: 18.4K). Weekly active dispatchers also beat target, at 6,120.
  • Activation is the miss. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points. Self-serve carrier onboarding, the other activation commitment, did not ship. All 4 churned accounts had raised onboarding tickets.
  • ARR grew 5.1% to $10.91M (Stripe), but net revenue retention was 96%. Churn ($1.11M) outweighed expansion ($741K).
  • Integrations are the top customer problem, and no plan addresses them. “No TMS integration” was the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals. Integration errors made up 30 of 55 support tickets. No bet or commitment targets this.
  • Delivery got faster but less safe. Deploys rose to 4.0 a week. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (range: under 5%) and review time to 13h (range: under 10h).
  • Inference spend ran 28% over plan ($612K against $480K), even though eval cost per task fell 20%. Most of this spend sits with one model provider (see risks).
  • Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot. The deadline was 4 Nov 2026.

Decision needed

Decision: Approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: Pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.
Deadline: 4 Nov 2026

Evidence: price was the stated reason in 6 of 17 lost deals in the CRM export. Per-load pricing is the pricing model the strategy already names (“We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.”).

This update is being issued on 25 Nov 2026, after the 4 Nov deadline. Nothing in the source files says whether the decision was made. If it is still open, it is overdue.

Strategy we're executing

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
Policy: We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
Actions: Raise agent task success to 90% · First automated load inside 14 days
Not doing: Build a carrier marketplace · Ship on-premise

Company objective: Grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). The product line is Dispatch Agent, at the Expand stage. The intake gives no target date for $50M, so we can't judge pace. ARR in Stripe is $10.9M.

Delivery health

Deploys rose from 3.0 to 4.0 a week, and restore time stayed in range. Two measures are out of range and getting worse: change failure rate (5.1% → 7.7%) and median review time (9h → 13h). Rework also rose (12% → 15% of PRs). The release checklist was meant to bring failures down. It shipped, but the failure rate rose.

Data gaps and caveats

Owner: the VP Engineering, to close by Q4.

GapEffect on this update
Lead time for change can't be measuredNone of the 60 Q3 pull requests in the GitHub export has a deploy time, so changes can't be traced to production. The PR open-to-merge figure (38h) is shown only as a proxy.
Stripe ARR and the finance revenue figure don't reconcileStripe shows $10.9M ARR at September. Finance close shows $10.3M revenue for the single quarter, which implies about $41M a year. Both can't be complete under their current labels. ARR figures in this update come from Stripe and may cover only part of the book.
Shipped commitments can't be traced to tracker itemsThe Linear export uses generic titles (for example “New capabilities item 1”), so the shipped/not-shipped status comes from the CPTO's intake, not from the tracker.
Team names don't match across sourcesThe intake names the Agent team, Activation team, Core dispatch and Platform. The tracker uses Core Dispatch team, Dispatch Agent team, Platform team. No “Activation team” appears in the tracker, so we can't see the effort behind the Guided activation bet.
Different eval setsTask success moved from 82% to 86% across two different eval sets (v13 and v14). If the cases changed between them, this is not a like-for-like comparison.
CRM and support exports have no datesWe can't confirm that the 39 deals and 55 tickets fall inside Q3.
Customer requests: one out of period, one unlinkedR-999 (dated 2026-05-02) falls outside Q3 and is excluded. R-007 has no link to a tracker item.
Investment mix is counted in itemsEvery tracker item has an estimate of 1, so the shares are counts of items, not effort.
