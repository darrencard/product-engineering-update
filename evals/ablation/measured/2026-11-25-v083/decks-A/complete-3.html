Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering Update

2026-Q3 (1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026) · Author: the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Compiled 25 Nov 2026 from system exports

Summary

  • The North Star beat target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100 a week, against a target of 22K. That's up 25.5% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers also beat target, at 6,120 against 6,000.
  • Activation missed. It reached 41% against a 50% target (Q2: 39%). Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship. Onboarding was the second-largest support theme, and all 4 accounts that churned had filed onboarding tickets.
  • Inference cost ran 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K). Unit cost improved: cost per eval task fell from $0.41 to $0.33. So the overrun probably comes from volume, and most of that spend sits with one model provider.
  • Delivery was faster but less stable. Deploys rose from 3.0 to 4.0 a week and lead time fell from 52h to 38h. But the change failure rate rose to 7.7% (range: under 5%) and review time rose to 13h (range: under 10h).
  • Sales and support point to a gap that no bet covers. 11 of 17 lost deals cited “No TMS integration”. “Integration errors” made up 30 of 55 support tickets. Neither Q4 bet addresses integrations.
  • A decision is past its deadline. The per-load pricing pilot was due for a decision by 4 Nov 2026. These files don't say whether that decision was made. See Decision needed.
  • ARR can't be confirmed from these files. Stripe shows $10.9M ARR. Finance shows $10.3M of revenue for the quarter alone, which annualizes to about $41M. Both can't be complete, so this update doesn't state progress toward the $50M ARR objective. See Data gaps.

Data gaps and caveats

Owner: the VP Engineering. Due: Q4.

  • ARR doesn't reconcile. Stripe shows $10.38M ARR in June and $10.91M in September (+5.1%, NRR 96.4%, GRR 89.3%). Finance shows $10.3M of revenue for Q3 alone, about $41M annualized. Either the Stripe export is incomplete or the two systems measure different things. Until this is reconciled, the update can't state progress toward the $50M ARR objective.
  • Lead time is a proxy. It's measured from PR open to merge, because the export doesn't link PRs to deploys.
  • Shipped status isn't verified. It comes from the intake interview, because the tracker export uses generic item titles.
  • Ship dates are missing for commitments, so I can't line them up against the timing of metric changes (for example, the release checklist against change failure rate).
  • Customer request KES-388 has no tracker link (R-007 and R-999), and R-999 is dated outside Q3.
  • The inference plan's volume assumption isn't in the finance export.
  • Cost per successful task is derived from eval-harness cost per task and pass rate. It isn't a production figure.
