Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering Update

2026-Q3 (1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026) · From the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Source: Linear export (100 issues), CPTO intake interview

Bottom line
  1. The team closed 100 tracker items in Q3. 31% were new capabilities for the Dispatch Agent, 26% improved the core Dispatch product and drove growth, 21% went to platform and scale, and 22% kept the lights on.
  2. Completions fell steadily through the quarter: 53 in July, 37 in August, 10 in September. In September, the only closed work was Dispatch Agent new capabilities (9 items) plus one keep-the-lights-on item. The Core Dispatch team closed nothing after 21 Aug, and the Platform team closed nothing after 2 Sep. The export can't tell us why (see §3).
  3. We can't yet judge whether Q3 was on track. The intake records no objectives, strategy, north-star metric, investment plan, risks or commitments, so there's nothing to measure delivery against. The VP Engineering owns closing these gaps in Q4.
  4. The Dispatch Agent (AI) has a named eval set, v14, but no eval results were provided. This update says nothing about its quality.
Read with care. Every item in the export has an estimate of 1 and status Done. So the counts here are item counts, not effort, and in-progress or planned work doesn't appear at all. The data stops at 30 Sep 2026, so nothing from Q4 to date is covered.

The drop is the biggest signal in the data, and the data can't explain it. Because the export contains only closed items, several explanations fit equally well:

  • work stalled;
  • teams moved to larger items that hadn't closed by 30 Sep;
  • teams were redeployed (for example, onto the Dispatch Agent);
  • or the export is incomplete.

Growth work stopped after 22 Jul, and keep-the-lights-on work stopped after 2 Sep. Both need an explanation: a production system with no maintenance work closed for four weeks is unusual.
