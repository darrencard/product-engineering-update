Kestrel Labs · Board update · Product & Engineering

Q3 2026: the agent is driving results. Activation and unit cost are the next constraints.

Period: 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared by the CPTO

Headlines

  • Automation is scaling. Loads cleared with no manual touch reached 23.1K/week, against a 22K target, up 26% on Q2. Every won deal cites the agent clearing exceptions.
  • Activation is the gap. 41% of accounts get a first automated load inside 14 days, against a 50% target. The guided activation experiment shipped, and its result is due in Q4.
  • Unit economics need attention. Revenue was $10.3M (plan $10.0M), but inference spend was $612K against a $480K plan. The agent's eval cost per task fell 20%, so volume is the likely driver.
  • Retention. In the billing export, four churned accounts ($1.11M) outweighed six new ones ($0.90M). Expansion still lifted ARR 5.1%, with net revenue retention at 96%.
