Summary
  • North star beat its target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week, against a 22K target and 18,400 in Q2 (+26%). Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120 (target 6,000).
  • Activation missed. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points from Q2. Guided activation shipped, but its expected 41%→50% lift has not shown up yet. The 4 accounts that churned ($1.11M ARR) had all raised onboarding tickets.
  • Integration is the biggest customer problem. Integration errors account for 30 of 55 support tickets. Those 30 accounts hold $8.38M of the $10.91M ARR in the billing export. "No TMS integration" accounts for 11 of 17 lost deals.
  • Release quality slipped while speed improved. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%), and PR review time rose to 13h (target under 10h). Lead time improved to 38h, and we now deploy 4 times a week.
  • Inference spend was $612K, 28% over its $480K plan. Most of it goes through one model provider, which is our top open risk.
  • The per-load pricing decision is overdue. Its deadline was 4 Nov 2026. These files don't record whether a decision was made (see below).

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot.

Options: Pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.

Deadline: 4 Nov 2026

The deadline has passed. As of 25 Nov 2026, the deadline was three weeks ago. The intake doesn't say whether the decision was made, so confirm its status before circulating. Supporting evidence from Q3: 6 of 17 lost deals cite price (CRM). The strategy already says we're "priced per load." Inference spend per no-touch load ran above plan (see Cost), which affects per-load margin.

R&D came in on plan. Inference ran 27.5% over. A rough measure of unit cost: Q3 inference spend divided by no-touch loads (23,100 × 13 weeks ≈ 300K) gives about $2.04 per no-touch load. The same calculation on plan figures gives about $1.68. This overstates true cost per load, because inference also serves loads that still needed a manual touch. Use it as a direction, not a unit cost. Q3 shipped Cheaper agent retries to lower this cost, but no export measures its target metric, cost per successful task.

Data gaps

These gaps prevent us from reporting on parts of our own strategy. Owner: the VP Engineering. Due: Q4.

  1. Agent task success. This is strategy action 1 and the Multi-stop reroutes bet's expected result (86% → 90%). It appears in no export, so we can't check the bet's stop rule (eval gain by week 3).
  2. Cost per successful task. This is the target metric for Cheaper agent retries. It isn't measured in any export.
  3. Guided activation live-test result. Without it, we can't apply the experiment's stop rule.
  4. Revenue vs ARR. Finance revenue ($10.3M for the quarter) and billing ARR ($10.9M) don't reconcile, so progress toward $50M ARR can't be stated.
