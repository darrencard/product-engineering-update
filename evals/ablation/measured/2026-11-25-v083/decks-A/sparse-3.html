2026-Q3 Product & Engineering Update

Source: Linear export of 100 completed items, data as of Sep 30 2026. From the CPTO. Prepared Nov 25 2026, about 8 weeks after quarter end.
Status: draft read by the CEO. The CEO signed off the board version.

How far this update goes. The only data available is a Linear export of completed items. The CPTO's intake left objectives, strategy, the north star, the investment plan, risks and decisions blank. So this update covers where engineering effort went and how it moved through the quarter. It doesn't cover outcomes, plan versus actual, or what customers got. Counts below are numbers of completed tracker items. Every item has an estimate of 1, so counts are the only size measure; they aren't hours or cost.

Headlines

  1. 100 items were completed in Linear in 2026-Q3. New capabilities, all on the Dispatch Agent, was the biggest share at 31%. Keep-the-lights-on came next at 22%, then Platform & scale at 21%. With no investment plan in the intake, there's no way to say whether that mix is on plan.
  2. Completions dropped sharply through the quarter: 53 in July, 37 in August, 10 in September. In September, 9 of the 10 were Dispatch Agent items. Three work types logged nothing after August: Growth on Jul 22, Improve the core on Aug 21, Platform & scale on Aug 30. The export doesn't show why.
  3. The Dispatch Agent's quality can't be reported. The intake names eval set v14, but no eval results were provided.
  4. Most of this update's usual content is missing. The intake has no objectives, strategy, north-star metric, risks, decisions or commitments. Only the tracker is connected. The VP Engineering owns closing these gaps, due Q4.

Every work type maps to exactly one product line, so these two views give the same split. Platform took the most items (43), but just over half of them (22) were keep-the-lights-on. Growth was the smallest stream at 8 items, and all of them closed in July.

Objective tagging. 78 of 100 items (78%) are tagged "Grow ARR to $50M". The other 22 are all keep-the-lights-on and untagged. That objective isn't in the intake and no ARR figures were provided. So the tag shows where work was labelled, not what it did for revenue.

Through August, every stream completed about one item every 3 days. Then three streams stopped logging: Growth on Jul 22, Improve the core on Aug 21, Platform & scale on Aug 30. Keep-the-lights-on stopped too, with its last item on Sep 2. From then until Sep 26, the Dispatch Agent team was the only one still closing items. Nothing was closed from Sep 27 to Sep 30.

What this data can't tell us: whether teams moved to work that hasn't closed yet (the export has only Done items), whether they were reassigned, or whether items just weren't updated. Each of those means something very different for Q4. This is the first gap below.
