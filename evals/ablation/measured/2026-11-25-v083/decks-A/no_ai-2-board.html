2026-Q3: the agent beat its north-star target; activation is behind

Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Author: the CPTO · Full leadership update: update.html

Headlines

  • The core bet is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week, against a 22K target (Q2: 18,400). All 22 won deals cite "Agent clears exceptions."
  • Activation missed. It reached 41% against a 50% target. The 4 churned accounts ($1.11M ARR) had all raised onboarding tickets.
  • Integration is the gap to close. It drives most support tickets and 11 of 17 lost deals ("No TMS integration").
  • Inference ran 28% over plan ($612K vs $480K) and is concentrated with one model provider.
