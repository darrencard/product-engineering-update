Decision requested

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter). Management recommends the pilot. Per-load pricing is central to the strategy, and price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals.

The original deadline was 4 Nov 2026 and has passed. The source files don't record whether the decision was made. Confirm the status before the meeting.

What happened

  • The agent is clearing more of the work. 6 of 7 product commitments shipped, including multi-stop reroutes. Agent task success reached 86% on the latest eval set (target 90%), and cost per task fell 20%.
  • Onboarding is the weak spot. Activation missed target, and self-serve carrier onboarding didn't ship. All 4 accounts that churned this quarter had filed onboarding tickets.
  • Integration is the main reason we lose deals. "No TMS integration" caused 11 of 17 losses and accounts for 55% of support tickets. No current bet addresses it.
  • Engineering ships faster, but quality slipped. Deploys rose to 4 a week and lead time fell to 38h. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%). The VP Engineering owns the fix, due Q4.
  • Spend: R&D came in on plan ($4.20M vs $4.245M). Inference ran 28% over plan ($612K vs $480K) as volume grew.

Top risk

One model provider carries most inference spend (likelihood medium, impact high). Mitigation: a routing layer across 2 providers, owned by the Platform lead, due Dec 2026.

Caveat on ARR

Progress toward the $50M ARR objective isn't stated here because the source systems disagree. Stripe sums to $10.9M ARR (net retention 96%), while Finance's $10.3M quarterly revenue implies about $41M a year. The two need reconciling before this figure is reported.

Next quarter

Two bets: push agent task success from 86% to 90% (stop if evals gain under 2 points by week 3), and lift activation from 41% to 50% (stop if a 2-week live test shows no lift). We stopped the carrier marketplace because it didn't serve the strategy.
