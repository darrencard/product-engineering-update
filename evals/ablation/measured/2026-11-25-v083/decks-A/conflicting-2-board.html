Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering, 2026-Q3

Board cut · 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Author: the CPTO

Headlines

  • ARR is $10.1M against an $11.0M plan. Churn ($1.11M, 4 accounts) cost more than new business ($0.90M, 6 accounts). Net revenue retention is 96%.
  • Customers buy for the reason our strategy predicts. All 22 wins cited "Agent clears exceptions". 11 of 17 losses cited no TMS integration.
  • The Dispatch Agent improved. Task success rose to 86% (goal 90%) and cost per task fell 20%. Six of seven quarterly commitments shipped.
  • Inference spend is 28% over plan ($612K vs $480K) and is concentrated on one model provider. A routing layer across two providers is due in Dec 2026.

The North Star sources disagree: PostHog shows 23,100 and the warehouse shows 21,400. Billing-system ARR ($10.91M) also differs from the finance close. The VP Engineering owns reconciling both in Q4. Until then, we are not reporting the North Star as met.

Decision

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (alternative: wait a quarter).
Why: per-load pricing is part of our strategy, and 6 of 17 lost deals cited price.
The requested deadline (4 Nov 2026) has passed with no recorded outcome. We ask the board to confirm the decision or set a new date.

Risks and focus for Q4

  • Integration gaps. They are the main reason we lose deals and the top support theme (30 of 55 tickets). No current plan addresses them.
  • Model provider concentration. Mitigation is the two-provider routing layer, due Dec 2026.
  • Activation. It sits at 41% against 50%. Guided activation shipped, but self-serve carrier onboarding did not.
  • Release quality. Change failure rate is 7.7% against a range of under 5%.

Stopped this quarter: carrier marketplace, which is a strategy non-goal.
