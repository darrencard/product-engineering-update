Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering update

2026-Q3 (1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026) · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Author: the CPTO · Audience: leadership

Summary

  • North star beat its target. 23,100 loads per week cleared with no manual touch, against a 22K target. That's up 26% from 18,400 in Q2.
  • Activation is the miss. It reached 41% against a 50% target, only 2 points above Q2. Guided activation shipped, but no lift shows up in these numbers yet. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped. All 4 accounts that churned had filed onboarding tickets.
  • Retention is below 100%. In the Stripe export, net revenue retention was 96.4% and gross retention 89.3%. Four accounts left, taking $1.11M ARR, which is more than the $0.90M that new accounts brought in.
  • Integrations are the strongest customer signal, and no bet covers them. "No TMS integration" was the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals. Integration errors made up 30 of 55 support tickets.
  • Inference cost ran 28% over plan ($612K against $480K), even though cost per task fell 20%. The overrun came from volume. Most of that spend goes to one model provider.
  • Engineering ships faster but with more failures. Deploys per week, lead time and time to restore are all within range. Change failure rate (7.7%) and review time (13h) are outside it.
  • One decision needed: the per-load pricing pilot (see below).
Decision: approve a per-load pricing pilot.
Options: run the pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers. Price was the stated reason in 6 of 17 lost deals in the CRM export, and per-load pricing is the pricing model the strategy is built on.
Deadline in the intake: 4 Nov 2026. That date has already passed (this document was compiled on 25 Nov 2026), and the inputs don't say whether the decision was made. Confirm its status before this goes out.

Strategy (unchanged)

Diagnosis. Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
Policy. We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
Actions. Raise agent task success to 90%. Get the first automated load inside 14 days.
Not doing. Build a carrier marketplace (stopped this quarter because it didn't serve the strategy). Ship on-premise.

Company objective: Grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). Product line: Dispatch Agent, Expand stage.

Why we win and lose (CRM)

22 won, 17 lost (56% win rate). Every win cites "Agent clears exceptions", which supports the strategy's diagnosis. Of the losses, 11 cite "No TMS integration" and 6 cite price.

What customers raise (support desk)

55 tickets from all 40 June accounts: integration errors 30, onboarding 18, billing 7. The four churned accounts (A037–A040) each filed one ticket, and every one was about onboarding. Four accounts is too few to call this a cause. Still, it points the same way as the activation miss and the slipped self-serve onboarding work.

For Q4 planning: integrations show up as the top loss reason and the top support theme. They're not a non-goal, yet no bet or commitment addresses them. Leadership should decide on purpose whether to fund them.
