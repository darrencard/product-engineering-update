What we learned

  • Win/loss: we won 22 of 39 deals (56%). 11 losses cited no TMS integration and 6 cited price. Integration errors are also the largest support theme (30 of 55 tickets).
  • Churn: four accounts ($1.11M ARR) left, and all of them had raised onboarding tickets. The self-serve carrier onboarding work slipped and didn't ship this quarter.
  • Cost: R&D spend was on plan ($4.20M vs $4.25M). Inference cost ran 27.5% over plan ($612K vs $480K), even though cost per agent task fell 20%.
  • Engineering: we're shipping faster (4 deploys a week, up from 3), but more releases are failing (7.7% vs a 5% ceiling).
