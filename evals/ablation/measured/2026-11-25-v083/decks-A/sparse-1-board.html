Q3 2026: most engineering work served the ARR goal, but output fell sharply by September and we can't yet show results

1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Author: the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Source: Linear export

  1. We finished 100 tickets. 78% were tied to "Grow ARR to $50M" and 22% kept existing systems running. The largest share, 31%, went to new capabilities, and all of it was the Dispatch Agent AI feature.
  2. Finished tickets fell from 53 in July to 37 in August and 10 in September. In September, 9 of the 10 were Dispatch Agent tickets. The VP Engineering is responsible for confirming whether this is a real slowdown, a change in priorities, or an incomplete data export. Please treat the trend as unconfirmed for now.
  3. We can report where the effort went, not what it achieved. We don't yet have a plan to compare against, outcome metrics, or Dispatch Agent quality results. The VP Engineering will close these gaps in Q4.
