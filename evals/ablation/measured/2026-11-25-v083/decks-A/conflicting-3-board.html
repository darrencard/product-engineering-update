What the board should know

  • The product thesis is holding. All 22 won deals cite the agent clearing exceptions. Task success rose 4 points to 86%, and cost per task fell 20%. Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120, beating the 6,000 target.
  • Revenue is behind plan. ARR was $10.1M against an $11.0M plan. We lost our four largest accounts ($1.11M ARR), which outweighed $0.90M from six new customers and $0.74M of expansion. The reasons for the churn aren't recorded yet.
  • Missing integrations are the main commercial gap. “No TMS integration” caused 11 of 17 lost deals and is the top support theme. It isn't covered by a current bet, so we'll take it into Q4 planning.
  • Inference cost is 28% over plan ($612K against $480K), even though unit cost fell. Most of that spend goes to one model provider. A routing layer across two providers is due in Dec 2026.
  • Execution: we shipped 6 of 7 commitments; self-serve carrier onboarding slipped. Deploys sped up, but the change failure rate (7.7%) and review times are outside our healthy ranges.
  • Two figures aren't reconciled yet. The north star reads 23.1K in one system and 21.4K in another, so we're not claiming the 22K target. Billing shows ARR $0.8M higher than finance close. The VP Engineering will reconcile both in Q4.

Decision

Per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative was to wait a quarter). Management recommended running the pilot. The decision was due 4 Nov 2026, and these materials don't record its outcome. It should be confirmed or recorded at this meeting.

Next quarter

  • Show the measured effect of Multi-stop reroutes (target 90% task success) and Guided activation (target 50% activation). Apply each bet's stop rule.
  • Decide whether integrations become a dedicated bet.
  • Bring the change failure rate back under 5%, and finish the inference routing layer by December.
