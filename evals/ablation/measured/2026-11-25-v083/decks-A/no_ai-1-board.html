Headline

The agent is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch beat target. But customers are slow to get to their first automated load, churn outweighed new business, and a missing TMS integration is the main reason we lose deals.

  • Delivery: 6 of 7 product commitments shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped.
  • Customers (Stripe export): 4 accounts churned, taking $1.11M of ARR. 6 new accounts added $0.90M. Net revenue retention was 96%.
  • Win/loss: we won 22 deals and lost 17. Of the losses, 11 cite no TMS integration and 6 cite price.
  • Team: 53 people across engineering, product and design, against a plan of 56. 3 roles are open.

Risks

  • Model provider concentration (on the register): one provider carries most inference spend. A routing layer across 2 providers is due in Dec 2026.
  • TMS integration gap (surfaced by this quarter's data): it's the top loss reason and the top support theme, and it isn't yet in the plan.
Data caveat. Finance reports Q3 revenue of $10.3M, but the Stripe export shows $10.9M ARR. These figures don't reconcile, so this update doesn't report progress against the $50M ARR objective.
