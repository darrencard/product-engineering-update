Product & Engineering Update: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · Prepared by the CPTO for leadership · Covers 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Summary

  1. The north star cleared its target. Our two data sources disagree on that. PostHog, our source of truth, shows 23,100 loads cleared per week with no manual touch, against a target of 22,000 and up from 18,400 in Q2. The warehouse shows 21,400, which is below target. This has to be reconciled before we treat the target as met.
  2. ARR is $10.91M against a $11.0M plan (99%), based on billing, our source of truth. That's up $531K in the quarter. Expansion and new logos added $1.64M, and four churned accounts took away $1.11M. The finance close reports $10.1M, so the two systems are $811K apart.
  3. 6 of 7 commitments shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship. Activation is 41% against a 50% target. All four accounts that churned had raised onboarding tickets.
  4. Our biggest product gap is TMS integration, and it isn't in the plan. 11 of the 17 deals we lost were lost because we had no TMS integration. Integration errors were the most common support theme, at 30 of 55 tickets.
  5. Inference spend is 27.5% over plan ($612K vs $480K), even though cost per agent task fell 20%. This adds to the risk that one model provider carries most of our inference spend.
  6. Two delivery measures are outside their target range. Change failure rate is 7.7% (target under 5%) and PR review time is 13h (target under 10h). The VP Engineering owns both, due in Q4.
Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot.
The options are to run the pilot with 10 customers now or to wait a quarter. We recommend running the pilot with 10 customers. Our strategy is to price per load, and price caused 6 of the 17 deals we lost in Q3.
The decision deadline was 4 Nov 2026, which has already passed. The source data doesn't record an outcome, so confirm the status before you act on this.

Strategy check

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle. Policy: We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load. Non-goals: building a carrier marketplace and shipping on-premise.

The market is confirming the diagnosis. All 22 deals we won (56% win rate) gave the same reason: "Agent clears exceptions." Our losses point at things the strategy doesn't currently cover:

Open gaps

  • Reconcile the north-star number between PostHog (23,100) and the warehouse (21,400).
  • Reconcile ARR between billing ($10.91M) and the finance close ($10.1M).
  • Bring change failure rate under 5% (now 7.7%) and PR review time under 10h (now 13h).
  • Report the week-3 eval reading for multi-stop reroutes and the 2-week live-test result for guided activation, so we can check each against its stop rule.
  • Set a metric for audit log export, and link customer request R-007 (carrier rating export) to a Linear issue.
  • Link the 22 keep-the-lights-on items to an objective, or explain why they took 22% of items against a 15% plan.
