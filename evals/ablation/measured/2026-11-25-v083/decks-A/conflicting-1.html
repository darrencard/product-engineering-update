Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering Update

2026-Q3 (1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026) · Author: the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Compiled 25 Nov 2026 · Audience: leadership
A shorter board cut is in board.html (signed off by the CEO).

Summary

  • We can't call the north star yet. PostHog has loads cleared per week at 23,100, above the 22K target. The warehouse has 21,400, below it. We are not reporting it as met until the two are reconciled.
  • ARR is behind plan, and the sources disagree. Finance close has $10.1M against an $11.0M plan (−$0.9M). Stripe billing adds up to $10.91M for September. Net revenue retention from Stripe is 96.4%: four accounts churned ($1.11M), 15 expanded (+$0.74M) and 6 were won (+$0.90M).
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments, but outcomes are mixed. Weekly active dispatchers (6,120, target 6,000) and agent cost per task ($0.41 → $0.33) improved. Activation (41%, target 50%) and agent task success (86%, target 90%) have not yet moved to their targets. Change failure rate got worse after the release checklist shipped.
  • Customers are telling us about integration. 11 of 17 lost deals cite "No TMS integration", and 30 of 55 support tickets are integration errors. None of this quarter's bets address it.
  • Decision overdue: the per-load pricing pilot needed an answer by 4 Nov 2026. The files don't record whether it was made.

Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.
Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. That date has passed. The source files don't record a decision, so leadership should confirm whether it was made and what was decided.
Why it matters: per-load pricing is the core of the strategy ("priced per load"), and 6 of 17 Q3 losses cite price.
