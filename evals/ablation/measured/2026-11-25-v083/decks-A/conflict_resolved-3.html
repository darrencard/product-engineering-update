Kestrel Labs · Product & Engineering update · 2026-Q3 (1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026)

Q3: the agent is clearing more loads, but it isn't keeping all of the accounts we win

Author: the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Compiled 25 Nov 2026 · Audience: leadership · A shorter board cut is in board.html (signed off by the CEO)

Summary

  • North Star is above target on its source of truth. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week in PostHog, up from 18,400 in Q2 (+25.5%). The 22K target is met. The warehouse reads 21,400, which would be a miss, and the two figures haven't been reconciled.
  • ARR grew to $10.91M (+$531K, +5.1% QoQ) in billing, $89K under the $11.0M plan. We added $900K new and $741K expansion but lost $1.11M to churn from 4 accounts, so gross retention was 89.3%. The finance close shows ARR at $10.1M, $811K lower than billing. That gap has to be closed before the next board.
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments, but two of them didn't move their metric. Activation only rose to 41% (target 50%). The release checklist shipped, yet change failure rate got worse, from 5.1% to 7.7%.
  • The agent got better and cheaper, but it's still at 86% task success against a 90% goal. Cost per successful task fell 19.5%, to $0.33. Total inference spend was still 27.5% over plan ($612K vs $480K) because volume grew.
  • The market keeps asking for integrations. "No TMS integration" accounts for 11 of 17 lost deals, and "Integration errors" for 30 of 55 support tickets. No current bet addresses it.
  • One decision is overdue. The per-load pricing pilot had a 4 Nov 2026 deadline, and nothing in the inputs records an outcome.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot.

Options: pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. This has passed, and the source files don't record a decision. If the pilot hasn't been decided, we're asking for it now. If it has, the record needs updating.

Why it matters: per-load pricing is part of the strategy itself ("priced per load"). Price was the stated reason for 6 of 17 lost deals in the CRM export. The export has no dates, so we can't tell how many of those losses fell in Q3.

Task success rose 4 points and cost per task fell 19.5%. These come from different eval-set versions (v13 vs v14), so the change is only roughly comparable. We're still 4 points short of the 90% strategic target.

Inference (cost of revenue) was $612K vs $480K plan (+$132K, +27.5%). Unit cost fell, but loads cleared grew 25.5%, which more than offset the savings. This also adds to the open risk that one model provider carries most of our inference spend (see Risks).
