Kestrel Labs — Product & Engineering, 2026-Q3 (Board summary)

Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared by the CPTO · The full leadership update has the detail.

Summary: The core strategy of automating dispatch exceptions is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch beat target, and every deal we won cited the agent. ARR grew 5.1% to $10.91M, but four accounts churned and net revenue retention was 96%. The gaps are activation, integration, and inference cost.

Three things to know

  1. Delivery: We shipped 6 of 7 commitments. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped. All four churned accounts had filed onboarding tickets. That's a small sample, but we're investigating.
  2. Integration is the biggest gap. Missing TMS integration was the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals, and integration errors were 30 of 55 support tickets. Nothing in the current plan addresses it.
  3. Inference cost and concentration. Spend ran 27.5% over plan, and most of it sits with one model provider. The mitigation is a routing layer across two providers, owned by the Platform lead and due Dec 2026.

Decision

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter). Recommendation: run the pilot. Per-load pricing is core to the strategy, and price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals. Deadline: 4 Nov 2026.
