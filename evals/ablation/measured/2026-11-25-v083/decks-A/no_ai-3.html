Kestrel Labs — Product & Engineering Update, 2026-Q3

From the CPTO to the leadership team · Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Sources: PostHog, Stripe, CRM, Linear (tracker and customer requests), Finance close, HR system, Incident tool, GitHub, Support desk, Developer survey, CPTO intake.

In short: The north star beat its target: 23,100 loads a week cleared with no manual touch, against a goal of 22K. ARR grew 5.1% to $10.91M. But we lost four accounts, which held net revenue retention to 96%. Activation is 9 points short of target. Inference spend ran 27% over plan.
  • Working: We shipped 6 of 7 commitments. Weekly active dispatchers passed 6,000. We deploy more often and restore service faster than last quarter.
  • Not working: Activation is 41% against a 50% target. Change failure rate is 7.7% against a goal of under 5%, and it rose even though we shipped the release checklist. Integration is our biggest problem with customers: it is the top reason we lose deals and the top support theme. No Q3 commitment addressed it.
  • Need from you: A decision on the per-load pricing pilot. Its deadline (4 Nov 2026) has passed, and these files don't record an outcome. See Decision.

Strategy, for context

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle. Policy: We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load. Actions: Raise agent task success to 90%. Get each new account to its first automated load inside 14 days. Not doing: a carrier marketplace (stopped this quarter because it did not serve the strategy) or on-premise deployment.

Company objective: Grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). We're at $10.91M, 22% of the way there. The intake gives no target date, so we can't say whether we're on pace.
