Product & Engineering: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · Board summary · Covers 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared by the CPTO

Headlines

  • The agent strategy is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch reached 23,100 a week, up 26% on Q2 and above the 22,000 target. Every deal we won this quarter cited the agent clearing exceptions.
  • ARR is $10.91M against an $11.0M plan. That's net +$531K in the quarter, with net revenue retention of 96%. Four churned accounts ($1.11M) offset most of the new and expansion revenue ($1.64M).
  • The largest gap is integration. 65% of lost deals cited the lack of a TMS integration, and it isn't in the current plan. Price accounted for the remaining 35%.
  • Activation (41%) and agent task success (86%) are both short of target (50% and 90%), even though the bets aimed at each have shipped.

Spend

R&D spend was $4.20M against a $4.25M plan, or 41% of revenue. Revenue was $10.3M against a $10.0M plan. Inference was $612K against a $480K plan (+28%). Cost per agent task fell 20%, but volume rose faster. Engineering has 44 people against a plan of 46.

Risks

  • We depend on one model provider for most inference spend (likelihood medium, impact high). The mitigation is a routing layer across two providers, due Dec 2026.
  • Without a TMS integration we lose deals and generate support load. There is no plan for it yet.
  • Delivery quality has slipped. Change failure rate is 7.7% (target under 5%). The VP Engineering will fix it in Q4.
Decision: approve a per-load pricing pilot.
The options are to pilot with 10 customers or to wait a quarter. We recommend running the pilot with 10 customers. Per-load pricing is core to the strategy, and price caused 6 of the 17 deals we lost in Q3.
