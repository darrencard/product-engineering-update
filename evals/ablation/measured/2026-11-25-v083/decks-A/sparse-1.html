Q3 2026: where engineering time went, and what we still can't see

Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026  ·  Author the CPTO  ·  Data as of 30 Sep 2026  ·  Prepared 25 Nov 2026  ·  Source Linear export (the only connected system)

In brief

  1. We finished 100 tickets in Q3. 78% were tied to a company goal and 22% were keep-the-lights-on work. The largest share, 31%, went to new capabilities, and all of it was Dispatch Agent. Next came platform & scale (21%), improving the core (18%) and growth (8%). The 78 goal-linked tickets all point to one goal, "Grow ARR to $50M".
  2. Finished tickets fell every month: 53 in July, 37 in August, 10 in September. By September, the only work being closed was Dispatch Agent plus one keep-the-lights-on ticket. Growth tickets stopped on 22 Jul, Improve the core on 21 Aug and Platform & scale on 30 Aug. The data doesn't say why. It could be a real slowdown, a change in priorities, or an incomplete export. We should find out before anyone quotes this trend.
  3. This update shows what we spent time on, not whether it worked. We have no Q3 plan to compare against, no north-star or outcome metrics, no Dispatch Agent evaluation results, and no risk register. Owner: the VP Engineering · due Q4

    Why the drop needs checking before it's quoted For every type of work, tickets were finished at a steady pace of one every three days, then stopped completely. They didn't taper off. Activity that stops this abruptly usually means one of two things: the export only includes some of the work (for example a date cut-off or a filter on project or team), or the teams deliberately moved off that work. The export contains only finished tickets, so we can't see whether anything was in progress or slipped. If the drop is real, it's the main story of the quarter. If it isn't, the July-to-September comparison is meaningless.

    3. Dispatch Agent (AI feature)

    Dispatch Agent is the only AI feature listed in the intake and the only product line still closing tickets through September: 31 new-capability tickets in total, 9 of them in September. The intake names its evaluation set as v14, but no results were supplied. We have no evidence on Dispatch Agent's quality, accuracy or customer impact this quarter. The tickets show it was built. They don't show it works well.

    4. What this update can't tell you yet

    The intake left these empty, and the only connected system is Linear, so none of them could be reported. Filling them in is assigned to the VP Engineering, due Q4.

    • Goals and the north star. No goals or north-star metric were provided. "Grow ARR to $50M" appears only as a label on tickets, so we have no target or progress figure for it.
    • Plan vs. actual. There's no investment plan, delivery ranges or commitments, so we can't say what was promised or whether it was delivered.
    • Outcomes. There's no product usage, revenue or customer data, so we can't link the 100 finished tickets to results.
    • AI quality. There are no results for Dispatch Agent's evaluation set (v14).
    • Risks, bets, things we stopped, and decisions. The intake has none recorded. That doesn't mean there aren't any.
    • Reliability and incidents. No operations or incident system is connected, so the 22 keep-the-lights-on tickets are the only sign of operational load.

    Decisions requested from leadership: none. The intake didn't submit any, and this update doesn't invent them.
