Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering Update

2026-Q3 (1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026) · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared by the CPTO for leadership · Compiled 25 Nov 2026

Timing note. This update is being compiled eight weeks after quarter close. The one decision it asks for, the per-load pricing pilot, had a deadline of 4 Nov 2026, which has passed. Nothing in the source files says whether it was decided. If it hasn't been, it needs a decision now.

Summary

  • The North Star beat its target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week against a 22K target, up 26% from 18,400 in Q2.
  • Activation is the gap that matters. 41% of accounts got a first automated load inside 14 days, against a 50% target. That is up 2 points on Q2. It is the metric behind the second strategic action. Guided activation shipped, but Self-serve carrier onboarding, the other activation commitment, did not.
  • Weekly active dispatchers are above target: 6,120 against 6,000, up 3.9% on Q2.
  • Inference cost is 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K), even though "Cheaper agent retries" shipped. We don't measure cost per successful task, so we can't tell whether that work helped.
  • We ship faster, but quality slipped. We now deploy 4 times a week (up from 3) and lead time fell from 52h to 38h. But the change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target: under 5%), even after the Release checklist shipped. PR review time rose to 13h (target: under 10h).
  • Integration is the strongest customer signal, and nothing in the plan addresses it. 11 of 17 lost deals cite "No TMS integration". Integration errors are the top support theme: 30 tickets from 30 of the 40 accounts that were live at the start of the quarter.
  • Revenue figures don't reconcile. Finance close reports Q3 revenue of $10.3M. The Stripe export shows $10.9M ARR at 30 Sep. These can't both describe the same business. See the data gaps section.

That's the only risk on the register. The data suggests three more:

  • Missing TMS integration is costing deals and loading support. This is the most common loss reason, and nothing in the plan addresses it.

The intake didn't assign owners to these. They're listed for leadership to triage, not as agreed risks.
