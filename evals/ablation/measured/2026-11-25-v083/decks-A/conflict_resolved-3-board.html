What happened

  • Usage is ahead of target. Loads cleared with no manual touch reached 23,100/week (target 22K), and weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120 (target 6,000).
  • Churn held back growth. New business ($900K) and expansion ($741K) were partly offset by $1.11M of churn. Net ARR was up $531K, to $10.91M.
  • We delivered most of what we committed, but not all of it worked. 6 of 7 commitments shipped. Activation is stuck at 41% (target 50%), and change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%).
  • Margins are under pressure. Cost per agent task fell 19.5%, but volume growth pushed inference spend to $612K, 27.5% over plan. R&D spend was 1.1% under plan.
  • The market is telling us something. Missing TMS integrations explain 11 of 17 lost deals and most support tickets. No current bet addresses this.

Decision requested

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter).

Recommendation: run the pilot. Per-load pricing is central to our strategy, and price was the stated reason for 6 of 17 lost deals.

The original deadline was 4 Nov 2026 and has passed. Management will confirm whether an interim decision was taken.
