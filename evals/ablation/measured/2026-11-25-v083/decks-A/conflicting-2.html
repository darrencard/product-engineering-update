Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering update

2026-Q3 (1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026) · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Author: the CPTO · Prepared 25 Nov 2026 · Audience: leadership team

Summary

  • ARR is behind plan. Finance close shows ARR of $10.1M against an $11.0M plan (−8%). The billing system says $10.91M. These two numbers have not been reconciled (see data gaps).
  • We cannot yet say whether the North Star hit target. PostHog shows 23,100 loads cleared per week, above the 22K target. The warehouse shows 21,400, below it. The sources differ by 1,700 (8%).
  • The strategy is holding where we win. All 22 deals won this quarter gave "Agent clears exceptions" as the reason. Losses point somewhere else: 11 of 17 were "No TMS integration", and integration errors are the top support theme (30 of 55 tickets).
  • Delivery shipped 6 of 7 commitments. Agent task success rose to 86% (eval v14), still short of 90%. Activation is 41% against a 50% target.
  • Cost and quality need attention. Inference spend ran 28% over plan ($612K vs $480K). Change failure rate is 7.7% (range: under 5%) and PR review time is 13h (range: under 10h). Keep-the-lights-on work took 22% of completed items against a 15% plan.
Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot.
Options: pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter. Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.
Evidence: 6 of 17 lost deals cited price, and the strategy commits us to per-load pricing.
The deadline was 4 Nov 2026 and has passed. The source files don't record an outcome, so this needs either a recorded decision or a new date.
