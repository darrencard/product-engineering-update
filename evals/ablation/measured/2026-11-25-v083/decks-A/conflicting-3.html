2026-Q3: the agent got better and cheaper; churn and integrations held revenue back

Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Author: the CPTO · Compiled 25 Nov 2026 from the CPTO intake and 14 system exports

Summary

  • The agent is improving. Task success rose from 82% to 86% (eval v13 → v14) and cost per task fell 20%, from $0.41 to $0.33. The 90% target hasn't been reached yet.
  • North star: two sources disagree, so I'm not calling it hit. Loads cleared per week was 23,100 in PostHog and 21,400 in the warehouse, against a 22K target. One source says we hit it and the other says we missed.
  • ARR is under plan, and the two systems disagree here too. Finance close says $10.1M against an $11.0M plan; Stripe billing says $10.9M. We lost 4 accounts worth $1.11M ARR, and they were the 4 largest in June.
  • Missing integrations are the biggest commercial drag, and no current bet addresses them. “No TMS integration” caused 11 of 17 lost deals, and “Integration errors” were 30 of 55 support tickets.
  • Delivery got faster but less safe. We deployed 4 times a week and lead time fell to 38h. But change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%) and review time rose to 13h (target under 10h).
  • Our 4 Nov decision deadline has passed. The decision on the per-load pricing pilot is overdue, and the files don't record an outcome.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. Options: pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.
Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. Passed. These files don't record whether a decision was made. If none has been made, it's overdue. If one has, this section should be updated to record it.

Why it matters: the strategy is “priced per load”, and 6 of 17 lost deals cited price (CRM). Note that the pilot doesn't address the larger loss reason, missing TMS integrations (11 of 17).

Strategy (unchanged)

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle. Policy: we win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load. Actions: raise agent task success to 90%, and get each new account to its first automated load inside 14 days. Non-goals: a carrier marketplace and on-premise deployment. We stopped the carrier marketplace because it didn't serve the strategy.

Company objective: Grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). All three product lines serve it: Dispatch Agent (Expand), Core Dispatch (Sustain) and Platform (Sustain).

Net revenue retention was 96% and gross revenue retention was 89%. Expansion was concentrated in 15 accounts (A001–A015), each up 20%. The other 21 retained accounts stayed flat.
