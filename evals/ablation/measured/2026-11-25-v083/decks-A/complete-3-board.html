Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering

Board update · 2026-Q3 (1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026) · Author: the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Headlines

  • The core agent bet is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100 a week, above the 22K target and up 25.5% on the prior quarter. Every deal we won cited the agent clearing exceptions.
  • Activation missed (41% against a 50% target). Onboarding problems are linked to churn: all 4 churned accounts had filed onboarding tickets. Guided activation is a Q4 experiment with a stop rule.
  • Inference cost ran 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K) as volume grew, and most of that spend sits with one provider. A routing layer across 2 providers is due Dec 2026.
  • The most common reason we lose deals is missing TMS integration (11 of 17 losses). No Q4 bet addresses it yet.
