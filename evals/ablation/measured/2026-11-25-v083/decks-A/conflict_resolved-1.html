Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering update, 2026-Q3

Period 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Compiled 25 Nov 2026 · Author: the CPTO
Company objective: Grow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO)

Summary

  • We hit the north star. We cleared 23,100 loads per week with no manual touch, against a 22K target. That is up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. One caveat: the warehouse reports 21,400 for the same metric, which would be a miss (see data notes).
  • ARR is $10.91M in billing, up 5.1% on the quarter but $89K short of the $11.0M plan. Four accounts churned, taking $1.11M. That is more than the $0.90M we added from new logos. Net revenue retention was 96.4%.
  • Activation is the biggest miss. It reached 41% against a 50% target. Guided activation shipped, but activation rose only 2 points.
  • Our plan doesn't address TMS integration. It explains 11 of 17 lost deals and 30 of 55 support tickets. No current bet covers it. The VP Engineering owns it, with a plan due in Q4.
  • Delivery is faster but less stable. Lead time fell from 52h to 38h, and deploys rose from 3.0 to 4.0 a week. But change failure rate rose from 5.1% to 7.7%, and review time rose from 9h to 13h. Both are now outside their target ranges.
  • Inference spend was 28% over plan ($612K against $480K), even though cost per agent task fell 20%. Load volume drove the overrun.
  • The pricing-pilot decision is overdue. Its deadline was 4 Nov 2026, and these files don't record a decision.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. The options are to pilot with 10 customers or to wait a quarter.
Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.
Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. This has passed. These files contain no record of a decision, so please confirm the outcome or set a new date.

Why now: 6 of the 17 deals lost in Q3 cited price. Per-load pricing is our stated policy ("automating the exception, priced per load").

Data notes

  • ARR. Billing (Stripe, the source of truth) shows $10.91M at September. The finance close shows $10.1M, so the two differ by $811K. This update uses billing.
  • North star. PostHog (the source of truth) shows 23,100. The warehouse (select avg(loads) from ns_weekly) shows 21,400, which is 7% lower and below the target. This update uses PostHog. Until the two are reconciled, treat the "hit" as provisional.
  • Timing. The data is as of 30 Sep 2026, and the update was compiled on 25 Nov 2026. Anything after the quarter end, including the pricing-pilot decision, is not reflected.
  • Delivery definitions. Each measure is computed from the GitHub export for pull requests merged, or deploys made, inside each quarter. Q2 runs from 1 Apr to 30 Jun and Q3 from 1 Jul to 30 Sep. Time to restore comes from the incident tool.
  • Sources. All 14 connected systems supplied data: tool admin exports, PostHog, Stripe, CRM, Linear (requests and tracker), the eval harness, the finance close, the HR system, the incident tool, GitHub, the support desk, the developer survey, and the warehouse.
