Kestrel Labs · Product & Engineering update · 2026-Q3

The agent is clearing more loads than planned. Activation, integration and change quality are behind.

Period 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Author: the CPTO · Compiled 25 Nov 2026. The data is about 8 weeks old, so later changes aren’t reflected.
Summary
  1. The agent is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week, above the 22K target and up 26% on Q2. Agent task success on eval set v14 rose to 86% (v13: 82%), and cost per task fell 20% to $0.33.
  2. Activation missed its target. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up 2 points. Guided activation shipped, but self-serve carrier onboarding did not. All 4 accounts that churned ($1.11M ARR) had filed onboarding tickets.
  3. Integration is the biggest gap, and nothing in the plan targets it. “No TMS integration” was the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals. Integration errors were also the top support theme (30 of 55 tickets). No Q3 commitment or current bet addresses either.
  4. Inference spend ran 27% over plan ($612K vs $480K), even though cost per task fell. Most of that spend sits with one model provider. The mitigation, a routing layer across 2 providers, is due Dec 2026.
  5. We ship faster, but quality slipped. Deploys rose to 4/week (Q2: 3). Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%), and review time rose to 13h (target under 10h). Both got worse after the release checklist shipped.
  6. Decision overdue: approve a per-load pricing pilot. The deadline was 4 Nov 2026. These files don’t record an outcome.
