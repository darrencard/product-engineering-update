What the board should know
  1. The agent is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week, above the 22K target and up 26% on Q2. Agent task success on eval set v14 rose to 86% (v13: 82%), and cost per task fell 20% to $0.33.
  2. Activation missed its target. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up 2 points. Guided activation shipped, but self-serve carrier onboarding did not. All 4 accounts that churned ($1.11M ARR) had filed onboarding tickets.
  3. For the accounts in the billing export, net revenue retention is 96% and gross is 89%. 15 accounts expanded and 6 new accounts were added. These figures don’t cover the whole customer base, so we aren’t reporting company ARR against the $50M objective here.
  4. Integration is the biggest gap, and nothing in the plan targets it. “No TMS integration” was the reason for 11 of 17 lost deals. Integration errors were also the top support theme (30 of 55 tickets). No Q3 commitment or current bet addresses either.
  5. Inference spend ran 27% over plan ($612K vs $480K), even though cost per task fell. Most of that spend sits with one model provider. The mitigation, a routing layer across 2 providers, is due Dec 2026.
  6. 6 of 7 commitments shipped. Engineering is at 44 of 46 planned. R&D spend was $4.20M, under the $4.25M plan.
