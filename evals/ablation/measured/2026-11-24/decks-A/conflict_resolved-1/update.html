Product & Engineering Update: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · Written by the CPTO for leadership · Covers 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

  • We beat the north star. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week against a 22K target, up 26% on Q2. That is the PostHog figure, which is the source of truth. The warehouse shows 21,400, which would be a miss. We have not reconciled the two yet.
  • ARR was $10.91M, about $89K short of the $11.0M plan. Net new ARR was +$531K, but we lost $1.11M to 4 churned accounts. Net revenue retention was 96%. Finance close reports a lower $10.1M, which also needs reconciling.
  • Activation is the weakest input: 41% against a 50% target. Guided activation shipped, and activation rose only 2 points. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship.
  • Delivery got faster but less stable. We deployed about 4 times a week and lead time fell to 38h. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (limit: under 5%) and review time rose to 13h (limit: under 10h).
  • Our unit costs fell, but total inference spend rose. Agent cost per task fell 20%. Inference still came in 28% over plan ($612K vs $480K) because volume grew. This makes the single-provider risk more urgent.
  • Integration gaps cost us the most deals. "No TMS integration" caused 11 of 17 lost deals, and integration errors were 30 of 55 support tickets. None of our current bets addresses this.
  • Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers.

1. Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: Pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why now: Our strategy is to win mid-market brokers with per-load pricing, and price caused 6 of the 17 deals we lost this quarter (CRM). The pilot tests that strategy directly. Waiting a quarter means we start Q1 still without pricing evidence.

Decide by: 4 Nov 2026.

2. Strategy recap

DiagnosisDispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
Guiding policyWe win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
ActionsRaise agent task success to 90% · Get each new account to its first automated load inside 14 days
Non-goalsBuild a carrier marketplace · Ship on-premise
Company objectiveGrow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). All three product lines serve it: Dispatch Agent (Expand), Core Dispatch (Sustain), Platform (Sustain).

The win reasons support the diagnosis. All 22 deals we won this quarter cite "Agent clears exceptions" (CRM).

12. Data gaps to close

The VP Engineering owns closing these gaps by the end of Q4.

  • ARR: billing shows $10.91M and finance close shows $10.1M, a gap of $811K. Separately, finance close lists Q3 revenue at $10.3M, which is close to annual ARR rather than one quarter's revenue. We need to confirm what that line measures.
  • Loads cleared: PostHog shows 23,100 and the warehouse shows 21,400. Whether we hit the target depends on which number is right.
  • Bet readouts: we have no week-3 eval for Multi-stop reroutes and no live-test result for Guided activation. We need both to apply the stop rules.
  • GitHub export: every PR within a quarter has the same lead time and review time. That pattern suggests the export is summarized or templated, so we should check the delivery numbers against raw data.
  • Tracking hygiene: Audit log export has no success metric, the KES-388 customer request is not linked to an issue, and keep-the-lights-on work is not tagged to an objective.
