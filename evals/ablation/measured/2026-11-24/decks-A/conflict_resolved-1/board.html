Product & Engineering: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · Board summary from the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Headline

The agent strategy is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose 26% to 23,100 a week, beating our 22K target, and every deal we won cited the agent. ARR came in at $10.91M, just short of the $11.0M plan. Our main problems are churn, slow activation, and a gap in TMS integration that is costing us deals.

Two figures are still being reconciled. Finance close shows ARR of $10.1M against $10.91M in billing. The warehouse shows 21,400 loads a week against 23,100 in PostHog. Billing and PostHog are our sources of truth. The VP Engineering owns closing both gaps by the end of Q4.

What moved

  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments. They include Multi-stop reroutes, Guided activation, and a 20% cut in agent cost per task. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped.
  • ARR grew $531K net. We added $900K from 6 new accounts and $741K in expansion, and lost $1.11M to 4 churned accounts.
  • We ship faster, but more releases fail. We deploy about 4 times a week, but 7.7% of releases failed, against a limit of under 5%.
  • We stopped work on a carrier marketplace because it did not serve the strategy.

Watch items

  • Integration gap: "No TMS integration" lost us 11 of 17 deals, and integration errors were 30 of 55 support tickets. We will weigh it in Q4 planning.
  • Inference cost and dependency: unit costs are falling, but total spend rises with volume, and one provider carries most of it. A routing layer across 2 providers is due in Dec 2026.

Decision requested

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers. The alternative is to wait a quarter.

Per-load pricing is central to our strategy, and price caused 6 of the 17 deals we lost this quarter. The pilot gives us pricing evidence before Q1. Decide by 4 Nov 2026.
