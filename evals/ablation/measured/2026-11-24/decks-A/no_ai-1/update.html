Q3 2026 update

Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 24 Nov 2026 · Author: the CPTO
Summary
  • The north star beat its target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100 a week against a 22K target, up 26% from 18,400 in Q2.
  • Activation missed. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up from 39%. Guided activation shipped, but there is no lift yet. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship.
  • ARR grew 5% to $10.91M (from $10.38M), but net revenue retention was 96%. Four accounts churned ($1.11M), and all four had raised onboarding tickets.
  • Integrations are the biggest gap, and no bet covers them. 65% of lost deals cited no TMS integration (11 of 17), and 55% of support tickets were integration errors (30 of 55).
  • Inference cost ran 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K), while volume ran only 5% over target. One provider carries most of that spend.
  • Delivery got faster but less stable. Deploys rose to 4.0 a week and lead time fell to 38h. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%), and PR review time rose to 13h (target under 10h).
  • We cannot yet measure agent task success, which is one of our two strategic actions and the expected outcome of our Promise bet. No export carries it.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why now: per-load pricing is the core of our strategy ("priced per load"), and 6 of 17 lost deals in Q3 cited price.

Deadline: 4 Nov 2026, which has already passed. If this has not been decided, it is overdue and needs a decision at this meeting.
