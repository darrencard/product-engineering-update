Kestrel Labs · Board update · Product & Engineering

Q3 2026

1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 24 Nov 2026 by the CPTO
The quarter in brief
  • The product is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100 a week, 5% above target and up 26% on Q2. Every won deal cited the agent clearing exceptions.
  • Retention and activation are holding growth back. ARR grew 5% to $10.91M, but churn of $1.11M cancelled out all of the expansion. Activation was 41% against a 50% target.
  • Integrations are the gap. TMS integration drove 65% of lost deals and integration errors were 55% of support tickets. It goes into Q4 planning as an open question.
  • Unit cost is rising. Inference ran 27.5% over plan, and one provider carries most of that spend. A two-provider routing layer is due in December.

Decision for the board

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. The options are to pilot with 10 customers or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers. Per-load pricing is central to our strategy, and price was cited in 6 of 17 lost deals.

The 4 Nov 2026 deadline has passed. If no decision has been made yet, it is overdue.

Delivery

We shipped 6 of 7 committed items. Multi-stop reroutes and the dispatcher inbox moved their metrics. Guided activation and the release checklist have not yet. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped. Engineering shipped more often (4 deploys a week, lead time 38h), but change failure rate rose to 7.7% against a target under 5%. Keep-the-lights-on work took 22% of capacity against a plan of 15%.
