Decision for the board

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter). We recommend the pilot. Per-load pricing is central to our strategy, and price was the reason for 6 of the 17 deals we lost.

The deadline was 4 Nov 2026 and has passed. The input files don't record the outcome. If it hasn't been decided, it's now overdue.

What went well

  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments, including Multi-stop reroutes (the feature customers asked for most) and Guided activation.
  • The agent got better and cheaper. Task success rose from 82% to 86%, and cost per task fell from $0.41 to $0.33.
  • Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120, above the 6,000 target. R&D spend came in 1% under plan at $4.20M.

What needs attention

  • Churn: 4 accounts churned, taking $1.11M (10.7% of opening ARR). All four had filed onboarding tickets. Activation is 9 points below target, and self-serve carrier onboarding, the one commitment we missed, targets the same problem.
  • Integration: No TMS integration cost us 11 of 17 lost deals, and integration errors make up 55% of support tickets. Our current strategy doesn't address it.
  • Spend and focus: Inference ran 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K), mostly because volume grew. Keep-the-lights-on work took 22% of engineering effort against a 15% plan.

Top risk

One model provider carries most of our inference spend (likelihood medium, impact high). We're adding a routing layer across two providers, due Dec 2026, owned by the Platform lead.
