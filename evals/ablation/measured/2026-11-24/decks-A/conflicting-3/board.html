Decision requested

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter).
Recommendation: run the pilot. Per-load pricing is the core of our strategy, and price was the reason given for 6 of 17 lost deals in Q3.
The deadline in the plan was 4 Nov 2026, which has passed. If the decision has not been made, it needs a new date.

What went well

  • 6 of 7 commitments shipped, including multi-stop reroutes, guided activation and the dispatcher inbox.
  • Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120, above the 6,000 target.
  • Agent task success rose from 82% to 86%, and cost per task fell 20% to $0.33.
  • Deploys rose to 4 a week and lead time fell to 38h. R&D spend was 1% under plan.

What needs attention

  • ARR is $0.9M behind plan. Net revenue retention was 96%: 4 accounts churned ($1.11M), and all of them had filed onboarding tickets. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship.
  • Integration gaps are costing deals. 11 of 17 losses cite no TMS integration, and integration errors are the top support theme.
  • Releases are less reliable. The change failure rate is 7.7% (target under 5%). The VP Engineering owns fixing this in Q4.
  • Inference spend ran 28% over plan ($612K against $480K), and most of it goes to a single provider. A routing layer across 2 providers is due Dec 2026.
