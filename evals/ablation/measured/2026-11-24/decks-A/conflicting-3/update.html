Kestrel Labs: Product & Engineering Update, 2026-Q3

From the CPTO · Covers 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Sources: PostHog, Warehouse, Stripe, Finance close, CRM, Linear, GitHub, Incident tool, Eval harness, Support desk, HR system, Tool admin exports, Developer survey
Summary
  • Usage grew; revenue did not keep up. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose from 18,400 to 21,400–23,100 a week (two sources disagree; see below). Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120, above the 6,000 target. Finance reports ARR of $10.1M against an $11M plan, and net revenue retention was 96%.
  • Activation is the weakest input. It reached 41%, up 2 points but 9 points short of the 50% target. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship. All four accounts that churned had filed onboarding tickets.
  • Delivery got faster but less reliable. We deployed 4 times a week, and lead time fell to 38h. The change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%), and PR review time rose to 13h (target under 10h). The VP Engineering owns closing this gap in Q4.
  • The agent is better and cheaper. On eval set v14, task success is 86%, up from 82%, and cost per task is $0.33, down from $0.41. Total inference spend still ran 28% over plan.
  • Decision needed: approve a 10-customer per-load pricing pilot. The deadline in the plan was 4 Nov 2026, which has already passed (see Decisions).

1. Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot.
Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.
Evidence: price was the stated reason for 6 of the 17 lost deals this quarter. Per-load pricing is our stated strategy ("automating the exception, priced per load"), and we cannot test it without a pilot.
Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. This date has already passed (today is 24 Nov 2026). If the decision has been made, record it here. If not, it needs a new date.

Integration is now the biggest theme across lost deals and support tickets, and our strategy actions do not address it. Leadership should decide whether integration counts as part of "automating the exception" or is a gap we accept.
