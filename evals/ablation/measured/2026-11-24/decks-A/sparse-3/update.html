Period 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30 · Data as of 2026-09-30 · Prepared for leadership, from the CPTO · Source: Linear tracker export (100 completed items)

Dispatch Agent kept a steady pace (12 → 10 → 9). The drop came from Core Dispatch, which had no completions in September, and Platform, which had one. This update covers delivery volume and mix only.
