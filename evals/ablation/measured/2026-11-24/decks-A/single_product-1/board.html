Product & Engineering — Q3 2026 Board Update

Kestrel Labs · From the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Decision: approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter).
Per-load pricing is central to our strategy, and 6 of 17 lost deals cited price. The decision was due 4 Nov 2026 and is overdue if still open.

The quarter in brief

  • The core thesis is working. Every won deal (22 of 22) cites the agent clearing exceptions. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week (+26%), beating the 22K target.
  • Revenue was $10.3M against a $10.0M plan. R&D spend was on plan at $4.2M. Inference cost $612K, 27.5% over plan, because volume grew faster than the 20% cut in unit cost.
  • Activation is behind: 41% against a 50% target. Missing TMS integration lost 11 of 17 deals and drives 55% of support tickets. Retention was 96% net and 89% gross. Four accounts churned, and every one had raised onboarding tickets.
  • Delivery got faster but less reliable. We now ship about 4 deploys a week, but the change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%).
