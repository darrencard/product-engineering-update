Product & Engineering Update — Q3 2026

Kestrel Labs · From the CPTO · Covers 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Summary

  • The agent is doing more of the work. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week, up 26% from 18,400 in Q2 and above the 22K target. Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120 (target 6,000).
  • Activation is the weak spot. It reached 41%, up from 39% but well short of the 50% target, even though Guided activation shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship, and all four accounts that churned had raised onboarding tickets.
  • Integrations are the main gap the plan doesn't cover. Missing TMS integration explains 11 of 17 lost deals, and integration errors account for 30 of 55 support tickets. Neither bet for next quarter addresses it.
  • Agent quality and unit cost improved, but total inference spend didn't hold. Eval pass rate rose from 82% to 86%, and cost per task fell 20% to $0.33. Inference still came in 27.5% over plan ($612K vs $480K), mostly because volume grew.
  • Delivery is faster but less reliable. Deploys rose to about 4 a week. The change failure rate climbed to 7.7% (target under 5%), and median PR review time rose to 13h (target under 10h).
  • Too much capacity went to keeping the lights on. It took 22% of completed work against a 15% plan, and the overrun came mostly out of new capabilities (31% vs 35%).
Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot.
Options: run the pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers. Per-load pricing is central to the strategy, and 6 of 17 lost deals cited price.
The decision deadline was 4 Nov 2026 and has passed. If the decision hasn't been made yet, it is now overdue.

Strategy, for reference

DiagnosisDispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
PolicyWe win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
ActionsRaise agent task success to 90%. Get the first automated load inside 14 days.
Non-goalsBuild a carrier marketplace. Ship on-premise.
ObjectiveGrow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). Product line: Dispatch Agent, Expand stage.

The policy is holding up in the market: all 22 won deals cite “Agent clears exceptions.” The main threats are the two actions. Task success is at 86% against a 90% goal, and activation within 14 days is at 41% against 50%.
