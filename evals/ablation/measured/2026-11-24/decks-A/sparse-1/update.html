Product & Engineering Update: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · from the CPTO · period 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30 · data as of 2026-09-30 · source: Linear tracker export (the only connected system)
Summary. We completed 100 tracker items in Q3. 78 of them are tagged to “Grow ARR to $50M”. The other 22 are keep-the-lights-on work with no objective. Completions fell each month: 53 in July, 37 in August, 10 in September. In September only the Dispatch Agent team was still closing work. We must confirm whether this is a real slowdown or a gap in the export before we draw conclusions. This update covers output only. No strategy, outcome, AI-quality or risk data was supplied (see the last section).
