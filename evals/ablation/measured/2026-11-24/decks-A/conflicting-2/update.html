Product & Engineering Update — 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · From the CPTO · Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 24 Nov 2026

Summary. The product is landing: usage and the agent's quality both improved, and revenue beat plan by 3%. But ARR is $10.1M against an $11.0M plan. Activation is stuck at 41% against a 50% target, and two delivery measures (change failure rate and review time) got worse. We can't yet say whether we hit the North Star, because our two sources disagree: 23.1K vs 21.4K against a 22K target. Inference spend came in 27% over plan.
  • Business: ARR $10.1M vs $11.0M plan (finance close). Billing shows $10.91M. The $0.81M gap needs reconciling. Net revenue retention 96%, gross retention 89%, 6 new accounts.
  • North Star (loads cleared per week, no manual touch): 23,100 in PostHog vs 21,400 in the Warehouse, against a 22K target. One source says hit, the other says miss, so we're reporting it as unresolved. Either way, it's up from 18,400 in Q2 (PostHog).
  • Inputs: Weekly active dispatchers 6,120, ahead of the 6,000 target. Activation 41% vs 50% target (39% in Q2).
  • Agent quality: Task success 86% on eval set v14 (82% on v13). Cost per task down 20% to $0.33. The Multi-stop reroutes bet aims for 90% and hasn't shown that lift yet.
  • Delivery: 4.0 deploys/week, and restore time is 41 min. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%) and median review time to 13h (target under 10h).
  • Why we lose deals: 11 of 17 lost deals cite "No TMS integration". Integration errors are also the top support theme (30 of 55 tickets). Neither current bet addresses this.
  • Decision: Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers. The stated deadline (4 Nov 2026) has already passed.

Supporting evidence from Q3 data: per-load pricing is the core of our strategy ("automating the exception, priced per load"). Price was the stated reason in 6 of 17 lost deals (CRM). Note that the bigger loss reason is missing TMS integration (11 of 17), and pricing doesn't fix that.

The deadline in the intake is earlier than this document's preparation date. If the decision has already been made, record the outcome. If it hasn't, it is overdue.

2. Strategy recap

Guiding policyWe win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
ActionsRaise agent task success to 90% · First automated load inside 14 days
Non-goalsBuild a carrier marketplace · Ship on-premise
Company objectiveGrow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). All three product lines roll up to this objective. Dispatch Agent is in Expand; Core Dispatch and Platform are in Sustain.

The data supports the strategy: all 22 won deals give the reason "Agent clears exceptions". Both actions are behind target, though: task success is 86% (target 90%) and activation is 41% (target 50%).
