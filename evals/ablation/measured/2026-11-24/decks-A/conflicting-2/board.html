What we learned

  • Integration is the biggest blocker. 11 of 17 lost deals cite "No TMS integration", and integration errors make up 55% of support tickets. No current bet addresses this.
  • Delivery is faster but less stable. Deploys rose to 4 per week, but change failure rate went up to 7.7% (target under 5%).
  • Agent unit costs fell, but total spend rose. Cost per task dropped 20%, while total inference spend ran 28% over plan.
  • 6 of 7 commitments shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped. All 4 churned accounts had filed onboarding tickets.
