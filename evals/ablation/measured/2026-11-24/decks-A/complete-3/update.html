Quarterly update: Q3 2026

Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · From the CPTO · For the leadership team
Summary
  • The north star beat target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23.1K/week, against a target of 22K. That's up 26% from 18.4K in Q2.
  • Activation is the miss. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points. Guided activation shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not.
  • The agent got better and cheaper. Eval task success rose from 82% to 86% (target 90%). Cost per successful task fell from $0.50 to $0.38. Total inference spend still ran $132K (28%) over plan.
  • Integrations are the biggest gap, and nothing on the plan addresses them. "No TMS integration" caused 11 of 17 lost deals. Integration errors made up 30 of 55 support tickets.
  • Delivery is faster but less stable. We deployed 4 times a week and lead time fell to 38h. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (range: under 5%) and PR review time rose to 13h (range: under 10h).
Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. The options are to pilot with 10 customers now, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers. Per-load pricing is our stated strategy. Price caused 6 of 17 lost deals this quarter.

Decision deadline: 4 Nov 2026.

Note on timing: This update was compiled on 24 Nov 2026 from data as of 30 Sep, so the 4 Nov deadline has passed. If the pilot hasn't been decided yet, it needs a new date.

1. Strategy we are executing

DiagnosisDispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
Guiding policyWe win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
ActionsRaise agent task success to 90% · First automated load inside 14 days
Non-goalsBuild a carrier marketplace · Ship on-premise
Company objectiveGrow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). All three product lines serve it. Dispatch Agent is in Expand. Core Dispatch and Platform are in Sustain.

The customer signal backs the diagnosis: all 22 won deals were won because "Agent clears exceptions".
