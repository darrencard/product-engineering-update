Product & Engineering — 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · Board update · from the CPTO · data as of 30 Sep 2026

The product is working, but revenue is not keeping pace. Loads cleared with no manual touch beat target at 23,100 a week, up 26%. ARR grew only 5% to $10.9M, and net revenue retention was 96%. A missing TMS integration is our largest blocker. It is not yet in the plan.

Decision for the board

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter). Recommendation: run the pilot.

Per-load pricing is our stated strategy, and we do not yet charge that way. Price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals. Inference cost rises with load volume and ran 28% over plan. The requested deadline (4 Nov 2026) has passed. If this has not been decided, we ask for a decision now.

What the board should know

  • The strategy is landing with customers. All 22 deals won cite "agent clears exceptions". 6 of 7 committed items shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped.
  • Integration is the gap. It caused 11 of 17 lost deals and 30 of 55 support tickets. No current bet or commitment covers it. It has no owner or plan yet.
  • Activation is not responding. Guided activation shipped and moved activation 2 points. The next phase has a hard stop rule: if a 2-week live test shows no lift, we stop.
  • Revenue growth is concentrated. 15 accounts expanded by 20% each, and 21 stayed flat. Churn cancelled most of the expansion.
  • Inference is a cost and concentration risk. Cost per task fell 20%, but volume grew faster. One provider carries most of the spend, and a routing layer across 2 providers is due in Dec 2026.
