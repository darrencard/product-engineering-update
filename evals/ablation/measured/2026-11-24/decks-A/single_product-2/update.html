Product & Engineering Update — 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · from the CPTO · period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · data as of 30 Sep 2026

Bottom line: The agent is working. Our North Star, loads cleared per week with no manual touch, came in at 23,100 against a 22K target, up 26% on Q2. Agent task success rose from 82% to 86%, and cost per task fell 20%. The business is not keeping up: ARR grew 5% to $10.9M and net revenue retention was 96%. Activation is stuck at 41% against a 50% target. A missing TMS integration is the top reason we lose deals and the top source of support tickets, and no current bet addresses it.

  • Hit: North Star (23.1K vs 22K), weekly active dispatchers (6,120 vs 6,000), deploy frequency, lead time, time to restore. 6 of 7 commitments shipped.
  • Missed: activation (41% vs 50%), change failure rate (7.7% vs under 5%), PR review time (13h vs under 10h). Inference spend ran 28% over plan.
  • Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers. The stated deadline was 4 Nov 2026, so it has passed. Please confirm whether this was decided.

1. Strategy recap

DiagnosisDispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
PolicyWe win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
Actions1. Raise agent task success to 90%. Now 86% (eval v14).
2. First automated load inside 14 days. 41% of accounts achieve this (activation).
Non-goalsBuild a carrier marketplace (stopped this quarter). Ship on-premise.
Company objectiveGrow ARR to $50M (owner: CEO). Product line: Dispatch Agent, stage Expand.

The strategy is holding up in the market. All 22 won deals this quarter cite "Agent clears exceptions". The losses are not about the agent. They come from integration (11 of 17) and price (6 of 17).

Integration is the largest gap the data shows. It causes most lost deals and over half of support tickets. It is not in the strategy's actions, the bets, or the commitments. It probably also drags on activation, because a broker cannot get to a first automated load without a working connection to their TMS. All 15 expanding accounts (A001–A015) filed integration tickets, so the problem affects our best customers as well. All 4 churned accounts (A037–A040) had filed onboarding tickets.

12. Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot.

Options: pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why: per-load pricing is our stated policy, but we do not yet charge that way. 6 of 17 lost deals cited price. Inference cost grows with loads (+28% over plan), and per-load pricing would tie revenue to the same driver.

The deadline was 4 Nov 2026 and has passed. If no decision has been made, we ask for one now.
