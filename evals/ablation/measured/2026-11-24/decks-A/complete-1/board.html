Decision for the board

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. We recommend running it with 10 customers now rather than waiting a quarter. Per-load pricing is central to our strategy, and price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals in Q3.
The 4 Nov 2026 deadline has passed. If the board has already decided, please record the outcome.

What went well

  • Our north star, loads cleared with no manual touch, beat target at 23,100 a week.
  • Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120 (target 6,000).
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments.
  • Expansion ($741K) and 6 new logos ($900K) more than offset $1.11M of churn.
  • Agent cost per task fell 20%. R&D spend came in 1% under plan at $4.20M.

What needs attention

  • Activation and churn. Activation reached 41% against a 50% target, and self-serve carrier onboarding slipped. All 4 churned accounts had raised onboarding tickets.
  • Integrations. Missing TMS integrations caused 11 of 17 lost deals, and integration errors made up 55% of support tickets. No current bet addresses this. Leadership will decide in Q4 whether to add one.
  • Inference cost. Inference spend ran 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K) because volume grew, and most of it sits with one provider. A routing layer across 2 providers is due Dec 2026.
  • Release quality. The change failure rate rose to 7.7%, outside the under-5% range we hold ourselves to.
