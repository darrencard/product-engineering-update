Product & Engineering Update: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · From the CPTO · Covers 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Summary

  • The north star beat target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100 a week against a 22K target, up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120 (target 6,000).
  • Activation missed. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points. Self-serve carrier onboarding, the second commitment tied to activation, did not ship. All 4 accounts that churned this quarter had raised an onboarding ticket.
  • ARR is $10.91M, up $531K (+5.1%) on June. Net revenue retention was 96%. Expansion ($741K) and 6 new logos ($900K) more than covered $1.11M of churn from 4 accounts.
  • Missing TMS integrations are our biggest problem, and our strategy doesn't address them. They caused 11 of 17 lost deals, and integration errors made up 30 of 55 support tickets. No current bet or strategic action targets integrations.
  • Delivery got faster but less stable. We deployed 4 times a week and lead time fell to 38h. But the change failure rate rose to 7.7% (range: under 5%) and PR review time rose to 13h (range: under 10h), even though the release checklist shipped.
  • Inference spend ran 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K). Cost per agent task fell 20%, so the overrun comes from volume. Most of that spend sits with one model provider, which is our top risk.
  • One decision is overdue. The per-load pricing pilot had a 4 Nov 2026 deadline (see below).

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot Deadline 4 Nov 2026 has passed

Options: Run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why: Our strategy commits to pricing per load. Price was the stated reason for 6 of 17 lost deals in Q3.
