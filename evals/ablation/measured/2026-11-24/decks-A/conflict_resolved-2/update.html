Product & Engineering Update: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · From the CPTO · Covers 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Written 24 Nov 2026

Summary

  • The north star beat its target. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100/week against a 22K target, up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. The warehouse puts it at 21,400, which would be a miss. That gap is unresolved (see Data notes).
  • ARR is $10.91M per Stripe, up 5.1% in the quarter and $89K (0.8%) short of the $11.0M plan. Net revenue retention was 96%: 4 accounts churned ($1.11M), which more than cancelled out $741K of expansion.
  • Activation is the weak spot. It reached 41% against a 50% target. Guided activation shipped but has not yet moved the number, and Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship.
  • Inference cost $612K, 27.5% over plan, while revenue was 3% over plan. Cost per agent task fell 20% to $0.33.
  • Delivery: 2 of 5 measures are out of range. Change failure rate is 7.7% (range: under 5%) and PR review time is 13h (range: under 10h). The VP Engineering owns the fix, due in Q4.
  • One decision is overdue. The per-load pricing pilot was due for a decision by 4 Nov 2026. These files do not show that it was made.

1. Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. Deadline was 4 Nov 2026, now past.

Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why now: the pilot puts the strategy's pricing model ("priced per load") into practice. Price was the reason for 6 of the 17 lost deals in the CRM export.

9. Customer and market signal

  • Win/loss (CRM, 39 deals): 22 won and 17 lost, a 56% win rate. Every win cited "Agent clears exceptions", which supports the strategy. Of the losses, 11 cited no TMS integration and 6 cited price.
  • Support (55 tickets): 30 were Integration errors (55%), 18 Onboarding and 7 Billing.
  • Requests (Linear): 7 requests arrived in Q3. 4 were for Multi-stop reroutes from Northwind, Haulwell and Bluepeak (shipped). 2 were for Bulk load import (KES-412) from Haulwell and Tidewater. 1 was for Carrier rating export (KES-388) from Bluepeak, and it is not linked to any work.

Takeaway: integration is the most frequent problem in this data. It is the top loss reason and the top support theme, yet no Q3 bet or commitment targets it. Onboarding friction showed up in the churned accounts and in the activation miss.
