What happened

  • Agent automation is working. Loads cleared per week with no manual touch rose 25.5% and beat target. Every won deal (22 of 22) cites the agent clearing exceptions.
  • Growth is leaking through churn. New business added $900K and expansion added $741K. Four churned accounts cost $1.11M, which is 10.7% of opening ARR. Net revenue retention for the quarter was 96.4%.
  • Onboarding is the bottleneck. Activation is still 9 points below target. One of the two activation projects didn't ship.
  • Six of 7 commitments shipped. R&D spend is on plan at $4.20M against $4.25M. Inference cost ran 27.5% over plan because volume grew, even though cost per task fell 19.5%.
  • Engineering ships faster but less reliably. We deployed 4 times a week, but 7.7% of releases failed, against a target under 5%. The VP Engineering owns the fix, due in Q4.
Decision: per-load pricing pilot. The recommendation is to run the pilot with 10 customers rather than wait a quarter. Price caused 6 of 17 lost deals.
The stated deadline (4 Nov 2026) has passed. The board should confirm whether this was decided, and decide now if it wasn't.
