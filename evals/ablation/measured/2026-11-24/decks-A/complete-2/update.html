Product & Engineering Update — 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · From the CPTO · Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 24 Nov 2026

Summary

  • The North Star beat target. Loads cleared per week with no manual touch averaged 23,100, against a 22K target. That is up 25.5% from 18,400 in Q2.
  • The agent got better and cheaper, but it hasn't reached the 90% goal. Task success on eval set v14 is 86%, up from 82% on v13. Eval cost per task fell from $0.41 to $0.33.
  • Activation is the weak input. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points from Q2. Guided activation shipped, but its lift doesn't show yet. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship.
  • Churn offset most of the ARR growth. Billed ARR grew +5.1% to $10.91M. New business added $900K and expansion added $741K, but 4 churned accounts took $1.11M. Net revenue retention for the quarter was 96.4%.
  • Integration is the largest gap in the strategy. "No TMS integration" caused 11 of 17 lost deals. "Integration errors" account for 30 of 55 support tickets. None of this quarter's bets addresses it.
  • Delivery got faster but less stable. We deployed 4.0 times a week, and PR lead time fell to 38h. But change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%) and review time to 13h (target under 10h).
  • Inference ran 27.5% over plan ($612K vs $480K). Most of that spend sits with one model provider, which is the top risk on the register.
Decision needed: approve a per-load pricing pilot.
Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter. Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.
Supporting data: price caused 6 of 17 lost deals this quarter. The strategy's policy is to price per load.
The stated deadline was 4 Nov 2026. As of today (24 Nov 2026) that date has passed. If the pilot hasn't been decided yet, it needs a decision now. If it has, record the outcome in the next update.

2. Bets & strategy

Diagnosis: Dispatchers lose most of their day to exceptions that rules engines can't handle.
Policy: We win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load.
Actions: Raise agent task success to 90%. Get each new account to its first automated load inside 14 days.

The win data supports the diagnosis: all 22 won deals cite "Agent clears exceptions". Both strategy actions are still short of target, at 86% task success and 41% activation.

Gap in the strategy: integration. "No TMS integration" is the most common loss reason, at 11 of 17 lost deals (65%). "Integration errors" is the most common support theme, at 30 of 55 tickets (55%). Integration isn't a bet, a stated non-goal, or a tracked customer request. Leadership should decide explicitly whether it is in scope for Q4.

9. Data gaps

Owner: the VP Engineering. Due: Q4.

  • Revenue doesn't reconcile with ARR. Finance reports $10.3M in Q3 revenue, but Stripe shows $10.9M ARR, and quarterly revenue should be about a quarter of ARR. Someone needs to confirm what the finance revenue line measures.
  • CRM wins vs new accounts. The CRM shows 22 won deals, but billing shows only 6 new accounts. The CRM and support exports also have no dates.
  • Lead time is partial. It covers PR open to merge only. The GitHub export needs a PR-to-deploy link.
  • Task success is offline only. It comes from evals alone, with no live production measure. Cost per successful task is derived from those eval numbers.
  • Stop rules can't be checked. There are no bet ship dates, no week-3 eval, and no live-test results.
  • Tracker completions drop sharply in September (10 items, vs 53 in Jul). This needs checking for an export cut-off.
  • Missing links and metrics. Requests R-007 and R-999 have no Linear link. "Audit log export" has no success metric. Platform "Keep the lights on" items have no objective.
