ARR is from finance close. The billing system shows $10.9M, and reconciliation is due in Q4. Loads cleared reads 21.4K in the warehouse and 23.1K in analytics, so we aren't claiming we hit the target.

What happened

  • Retention held ARR back. Expansion added $0.74M and 6 new accounts added $0.90M, but 4 churned accounts took out $1.11M. Net revenue retention was 96%.
  • The product is working where customers use it. Loads cleared with no manual touch grew 16–26% from Q2, depending on the source. Weekly active dispatchers reached 6,120, above the 6,000 target. Every won deal cited the agent clearing exceptions.
  • We shipped 6 of 7 commitments, including both bets: multi-stop reroutes and guided activation. Neither has a measured result yet. Self-serve carrier onboarding slipped.
  • Integration is the gap we haven't funded. Missing TMS integration explains 11 of 17 lost deals, and integration errors are over half of support tickets. We'll bring an integration proposal to Q4 planning.
