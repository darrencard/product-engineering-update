Product & Engineering Update: Q3 2026

Kestrel Labs · From the CPTO · Period 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared 24 Nov 2026

Summary

  • ARR came in below plan. Finance close puts ARR at $10.1M against an $11.0M plan (8% short). The billing export shows $10.9M instead. The two sources don't agree yet, but both are short of plan. In billing, 4 churned accounts took out $1.11M, which was more than the $0.74M of expansion. Net revenue retention was 96%.
  • We can't confirm we hit the north star. Loads cleared per week reads 21.4K in the warehouse and 23.1K in PostHog, against a 22K target. One source says we hit it and the other says we missed. Either way it grew strongly from 18.4K in Q2.
  • Activation is the weakest input: 41% against a 50% target, up 2 points. Guided activation shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not.
  • The agent improved, but inference cost more than planned. Eval task success rose from 82% to 86% (v13 to v14), and eval cost per task fell 20% to $0.33. Inference spend was $612K against a $480K plan (+28%).
  • Delivery got faster, but quality slipped. Deploys went from 3 to 4 a week and lead time from 52h to 38h. But change failure rate is 7.7%, above the 5% target range, and review time is 13h, above the 10h range.
  • Integration is the biggest customer pain we haven't funded. Missing TMS integration explains 11 of 17 lost deals, and integration errors are 30 of 55 support tickets. No current bet addresses it.

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. The options are to run a pilot with 10 customers or to wait a quarter.
Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why: our strategy is to win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load. Today's pricing doesn't match that. Price was the reason for 6 of 17 lost deals in Q3.

Deadline was 4 Nov 2026. That date has passed as of this writing, and the source files don't record an outcome. If the decision was made, record it here. If it wasn't, the pilot is already late.

Data notes and reconciliation

These conflicts and gaps affect the numbers above. Owner: the VP Engineering. Due: Q4.

  1. ARR. Finance close says $10.1M and billing says $10.91M, a $0.81M gap. Finance is also below June's billing figure of $10.38M, while billing shows growth.
  2. North star. The warehouse says 21.4K and PostHog says 23.1K. Whether we hit the 22K target depends on which one is right. We need one agreed source and query.
  3. Revenue line. Finance close reports Q3 revenue of $10.3M against a $10.0M plan. That is about equal to total ARR, which can't be quarterly revenue for a subscription business. It may be annualized or year-to-date. This update doesn't use it.
  4. Bet readouts. We have no weekly evals for multi-stop reroutes and no live-test result for guided activation, so we can't check either stop rule.
  5. Lead time. We measure PR opened to merged because the export doesn't link PRs to deploys.
  6. Effort. Every tracker item has an estimate of 1, so the effort mix is based on item counts.
  7. Deals and new accounts. The CRM has 22 won deals, but billing shows 6 new accounts.
  8. Cost per task. The only figure comes from the eval harness, not production.
