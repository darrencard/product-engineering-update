What the board should know

  • The product thesis is holding. The north star beat target. All 22 won deals cited the agent clearing exceptions. Retained accounts expanded by about 20% in the quarter. 6 of 7 commitments shipped.
  • Churn offset growth. New logos added $900K and expansion added $741K, but churn removed $1.11M. All 4 churned accounts had onboarding issues. The fix is to get accounts to their first automated load faster: activation is our top Q4 priority.
  • Integrations are an open gap. Missing TMS integrations cost 11 of 17 lost deals and account for 55% of support tickets. No current bet addresses them, and that is a Q4 planning decision.
  • Spend is on plan except inference. R&D came in at $4.20M against a $4.25M plan. Inference ran 27.5% over plan at $612K, partly because agent volume grew.
  • Engineering throughput is up and quality is down. We deploy 4 times a week (previously 3), but change failure rate rose to 7.7% against a target under 5%.
