Q3 2026 update for leadership

Period 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Prepared by the CPTO
Summary
  • North star beat target. Loads cleared per week with no manual touch averaged 23,100 (target 22K), up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers also beat target at 6,120 (target 6,000).
  • Activation is the gap. It reached 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points even though Guided activation shipped. All 4 accounts that churned this quarter had filed onboarding tickets, and Self-serve carrier onboarding, the only commitment we missed, also targets activation.
  • ARR grew 5.1% to $10.91M (Stripe). Expansion added $741K, new logos added $900K and churn removed $1.11M, so net revenue retention was 96.4%. Missing TMS integrations cost us 11 of 17 lost deals.
  • Delivery got faster but less stable. Deploys went up to 4 a week and lead time dropped to 38h. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%) and PR review time rose to 13h (target under 10h).
  • Inference spend ran 27.5% over plan ($612K vs $480K). Everything else in R&D came in at or under plan.
Decision needed · deadline 4 Nov 2026
Approve a per-load pricing pilot

Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.
Recommendation: Run the pilot with 10 customers.

Why now: per-load pricing is central to our strategy ("we win mid-market brokers by automating the exception, priced per load"). Price was the reason for 6 of the 17 lost deals in the CRM export. The 4 Nov deadline has passed relative to the date this update was compiled. If the decision has already been made, record the outcome here. If not, make it at this meeting.
