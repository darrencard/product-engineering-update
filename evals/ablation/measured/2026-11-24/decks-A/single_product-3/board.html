Product & Engineering: 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · Board summary from the CPTO · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Headline

The agent is working. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose 26% to 23,100 a week, ahead of target. Revenue isn't following fast enough. ARR grew 5.1% to $10.91M, churn outran expansion, and the $50M objective is not on track at this pace. Q4 focuses on activation, pricing and cost control.

Decision for the board

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers (the alternative is to wait a quarter).
Why: the strategy is to price per load, and price was the reason in 6 of 17 lost deals. Inference spend works out to at most about $2.04 per load cleared, which gives a cost floor for pricing. The original deadline was 4 Nov 2026, so this decision is overdue.

What the board should know

  • We ship reliably. 6 of 7 Q3 commitments shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not.
  • Onboarding and integration are the problem. Missing TMS integration lost 11 of 17 deals, integration errors are 30 of 55 support tickets, and every churned account had filed onboarding tickets.
  • Inference is the cost to watch. Unit cost per task fell 20%, but total spend ran 28% over plan as volume grew. Most of it sits with one provider. A second provider routes traffic by Dec 2026.
  • Delivery quality slipped. Change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%), and maintenance work took 22% of capacity against 15% planned. R&D spend was on plan at $4.20M.
  • We stayed focused. We stopped the carrier marketplace because it didn't serve the strategy.
