Product & Engineering — 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · Board update from the CPTO · Covers 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026

Headline

Our automation is landing with customers. Loads cleared with no manual touch rose 26% to 23,100 a week, beating target. Revenue came in 3% above plan. But activation is short of target, inference costs are running ahead of plan, and missing TMS integrations are the main reason we lose deals.

We shipped 6 of 7 product commitments. The one that didn't ship was Self-serve carrier onboarding. Engineering headcount is 44 against a plan of 46.

Decision for the board

Approve a per-load pricing pilot with 10 customers. The alternative is to wait a quarter. The deadline was 4 Nov 2026, which has passed. If no decision has been made yet, it is overdue.

Why now: pricing per load is central to our strategy, and price caused 6 of 17 lost deals in Q3. Q3 inference cost came to about $2.04 per cleared load, against a plan of $1.68, which sets the floor any per-load price has to clear.

What we're watching

  • Integration. 65% of lost deals cited "No TMS integration", and 55% of support tickets were integration errors. None of our current bets addresses it. We will bring a recommendation to Q4 planning.
  • Inference cost and model-provider concentration. One provider carries most of our inference spend. We are building a routing layer across 2 providers, due Dec 2026.
  • Activation and onboarding. Activation is 9 points below target. The four accounts that churned ($1.11M ARR) had all opened onboarding tickets.
  • Release quality. We are shipping faster (4 deploys a week), but change failure rate rose to 7.7%, above our 5% target.

Caveats

We can't yet report agent task success or cost per successful task. The VP Engineering owns closing those gaps in Q4. The billing export's ARR ($10.9M) doesn't match Finance's quarterly revenue ($10.3M), so this update doesn't report progress toward the $50M ARR objective until the two are reconciled.
