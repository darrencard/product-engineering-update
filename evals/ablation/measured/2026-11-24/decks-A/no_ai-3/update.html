Product & Engineering Update — 2026-Q3

Kestrel Labs · From the CPTO · Covers 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 · Data as of 30 Sep 2026 · Audience: leadership team

Summary

  • We beat the North Star. Loads cleared with no manual touch averaged 23,100 a week, against a 22K target. That is up 26% from 18,400 in Q2. Weekly active dispatchers also beat target at 6,120 (target 6,000).
  • We missed activation. It is at 41% against a 50% target, up only 2 points from Q2, even though Guided activation shipped. Self-serve carrier onboarding did not ship. Onboarding is also the second-biggest support theme, and the four accounts that churned had all opened onboarding tickets.
  • Integration is our biggest problem, and none of our bets addresses it. 11 of 17 lost deals (65%) cited "No TMS integration", and 30 of 55 support tickets (55%) were integration errors.
  • Inference cost ran 27.5% over plan ($612K against $480K) while loads grew. We have no cost-per-successful-task figure yet, so we can't tell whether Cheaper agent retries worked.
  • Delivery got faster but less stable. Deploys rose to 4 a week and lead time improved, but change failure rate rose to 7.7% (target under 5%) and review time rose to 13h (target under 10h).
  • One decision is overdue: the per-load pricing pilot had a 4 Nov 2026 deadline. If it is still open, it needs a decision now (see next section).

Decision needed

Approve a per-load pricing pilot. Deadline: 4 Nov 2026. That date has passed as of this writing (24 Nov). If no decision has been made, it is overdue.

Options: run a pilot with 10 customers, or wait a quarter.

Recommendation: run the pilot with 10 customers.

Supporting data: per-load pricing is the core of our strategy. Price caused 6 of 17 lost deals in Q3. As a rough cost floor, Q3 inference cost works out to about $2.04 per cleared load ($612K ÷ 23,100 × 13 weeks), against a plan of about $1.68. That figure is an upper bound, because inference also serves loads that still needed a manual touch.
