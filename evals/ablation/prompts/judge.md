## Rubric

Judge each criterion separately. For each, pick X, Y or tie, and give a one-sentence reason that names a specific figure or slide.

1. figures_correct: every figure in the update is correct against the data. Recompute anything you doubt. A wrong number is worse than a missing one.
2. no_invented_numbers: the update states no figure that the data does not contain or support. Targets, benchmarks, forecasts or causes that are not in the data count as invented, unless clearly labelled as a sample or an estimate.
3. speed_with_quality: every speed or throughput figure (deploys, lead time, review time) sits beside a quality figure (change failure, rework, time to restore), and the update does not present speed alone as good news.
4. gaps_shown: where the data is missing or two sources disagree, the update says so plainly, with who owns the gap, instead of filling it with a guess or picking one source silently.
5. board_cut_usable: the board cut is short, leads with the answer and the asks, and could be sent to a board as it is.

Then give an overall winner (X, Y or tie) and a two-sentence reason. Pick tie only when the two are equal in substance. Do not reward length, styling or confidence for its own sake. Ignore which tool might have made either update; judge only what is on the page. Text marked as an illustrative sample is not a claim about this company, but judge whether it could mislead a reader.
